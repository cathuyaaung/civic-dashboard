15.3
A Notice of Motion regarding Canada Day Funding for Business Improvement Areas (BIAs) (Councillor Dasko)
1. Motion - Canada Day Funding 2026 formatted.pdf
This matter was discussed and voted on under item 7.1.
RESOLUTION 0039-2026
Moved By Councillor S. Dasko
Seconded By Councillor N. Hart
WHEREAS Canada Day is a day of immense significance, which celebrates the birth of our country and commemorates the rich history, diverse culture, and shared values of
Canada and Canadians, from coast to coast;
AND WHEREAS spectacular Canada Day celebrations and festivities are held across our country and city each year, where Canadians patriotically gather together and unite
to celebrate our shared heritage and take pride in our great country;
AND WHEREAS Port Credit Business Improvement Area (BIA), Malton Business Improvement Area (BIA), and Streetsville Business Improvement Area (BIA) all have planned Canada Day celebrations to support this most important day in 2026;
AND WHEREAS Port Credit BIA applied for and was successful in attaining city grant funding through the Cultural Festivals and Celebrations Grant Program; however, Port Credit BIA was awarded $137,000 of their requested $197,627, leaving a shortfall in these BIA’s event budgets;
AND WHEREAS Malton BIA applied for and was successful in attaining city grant funding through the Cultural Festivals and Celebrations Grant Program; however, Malton BIA was awarded $15,000 of its requested $27,000, leaving a shortfall in these BIA’s event budgets;
AND WHEREAS Streetsville BIA applied for and was successful in attaining city grant funding through the Cultural Festivals and Celebrations Grant Program – however, Streetsville BIA was awarded $20,000 of their requested $40,000, leaving a shortfall in these BIA’s event budgets;
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT
1. Port Credit BIA receives additional direct funding in order to cover anticipated event costs from the City’s portion of the Municipal Accommodation Tax (MAT) fund in the amount of $60,627.
2. Malton BIA receives additional direct funding in order to cover anticipated event costs from the City’s portion of the Municipal Accommodation Tax (MAT) fund in the amount of $12,000
3. Streetsville BIA receives additional direct funding in order to cover anticipated event costs from the City’s portion of the Municipal Accommodation Tax (MAT) fund in the amount of $20,000.
Staff be directed to review and bring forward a report reviewing the commitment the city makes towards Canada Day across the city, including but not limited to reviewing potential alternative funding supports that can be provided as an alternative to established grant process for 2027 and beyond.

21.1
Personal matters about an identifiable individual, including municipal or local board employees: Renaming Riverview Park (Ward 11)
Public Comments:
Councillor Butt spoke to the subject matter and Members of Council spoke to the renaming process. Andrew Whittemore, Commissioner, Planning Building responded to questions.
RESOLUTION 0045-2026
Moved By Councillor B. Butt
Seconded By Councillor J. Horneck
WHEREAS Mr. Ralph Hunter is a long-time Mississauga resident, who has dedicatedly served the Village of Streetsville, City of Mississauga, and Region of Peel through exceptional public and volunteer community service work for over six decades;
AND WHEREAS Mr. Hunter has undertaken exceptional and extensive public service work for many decades, that has significantly benefited the Village of Streetsville, City of Mississauga, and Region of Peel, giving his time and expertise to serve on the Streetsville Planning Board (1963-1965); Streetsville Parks and Recreation Board (1971-1974, Director, Chair); Peel Police Service Board (2003-2008, Director, Vice Chair); and Mississauga Living Arts Centre Board (1997-2016, Director, Vice Chair);
AND WHEREAS Mr. Hunter has undertaken extensive community service volunteer work, enriching and benefiting the Village of Streetsville and City of Mississauga for several decades, including serving with the Streetsville Kinsmen (1960-1968, Editor, Director, Secretary, VP); Streetsville United Church (1965-1973, Board of Stewards, Chair); Streetsville Rotary Club (1969-present, Director, Secretary, Editor, VP, President); Streetsville Cancer Society (1975- 1978, Canvas Chair); Safe City Mississauga (2010-2014, Director, Chair); and Streetsville Probus Club (2011-present, Rotary Committee Chair, Founder);
AND WHEREAS Mr. Hunter has been a prominent Mississauga business leader for over six decades, establishing several successful local businesses and providing employment in the Village of Streetsville and City of Mississauga, including Hunter Insurance Inc. (1960-2000); Streetsville Glen Golf Course Inc. (1970-1990); Octus Investments Ltd. (1971-1977); Trakenmar Inc. (1977-present); and Hunter Gray Financial Services Inc. (1994-2013);
AND WHEREAS in recognition of his extensive and exceptional public and community service, Mr. Hunter has received several awards and honours, including the prestigious Paul Harris Fellowship Award from Streetsville Rotary (1970) for outstanding community service; and the Queen Elizabeth II Golden Jubilee Medal (2002) and Queen Elizabeth II Diamond Jubilee Medal (2012) for outstanding and exemplary contributions and service to the community and country;
AND WHEREAS Mr. Hunter’s exceptional community service and significant contributions to the Village of Streetsville, City of Mississauga, and Region of Peel, which are indicated above, should be duly honoured and recognized in the community he dedicatedly served for over six decades;
AND WHEREAS in recognition of Mr. Hunter’s exceptional community service and significant contributions to the Village of Streetsville, City of Mississauga, and Region of Peel, it would be fitting and appropriate to allow for the renaming of Riverview Park (1735 Britannia Rd W), which is located in the Ward 11 community and in close proximity to Mr. Hunter’s longtime residence, to Ralph Hunter Park;
AND WHEREAS there are several examples of City assets being named after identifiable living individuals who have made significant contributions to our City, such as the Culham Trail, Haroon Khan Trail, Maja Prentice Theatre, Dr. Martin L. Dobkin Community Park, Paul Coffey Park, Paul Henderson Dr., and Jim Murray Community Park, etc.;
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT:
1. Riverview Park, located in the Ward 11 community, be renamed to Ralph Hunter Park, in honour and recognition of long-time Mississauga resident, Mr. Ralph Hunter;
2. The appropriate new park signage be installed at Ralph Hunter Park.
