6.1
Growing Healthy Places: Mississauga
Sandy Duggan, Program Delivery Lead, provided an overview of Growing Healthy Places: Mississauga , a new initiative designed to support the well‑being of school‑aged children in the Region of Peel. Ms. Duggan explained that over the next two years, selected schools will work with local partners to increase access to nutritious food for children aged 6 to 13 and to promote healthy eating and physical activity. Ms. Duggan noted that this multi‑organization initiative is part of a global effort across six international cities, with Mississauga being the only Canadian city selected.
Mayor Parrish provided an overview of the creation of the Malton Youth Hub – Jonathan Davis Centre (HUB), highlighting the programs and services offered and expressed appreciation to all partners involved.
In response to Councillor Hart’s enquiry regarding sustainable funding models for food sourcing, Ms. Duggan advised that the team is working with school boards that already operate nutrition programs to identify available funding sources and is also seeking volunteers to support program delivery.
In response to Councillor Fonseca’s enquiry, Ms. Duggan explained that the project is being shared globally through partner communications and social media, and she referenced upcoming webinars with partner cities and global collaborators in which Mississauga will be featured.
Mayor Parrish added that, despite Food Banks Mississauga’s policy on not providing food to groups organized by a level of government, Mayor Parrish will work with the organization to explore options to support food delivery to the HUB.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0107-2026
Moved By Mayor Parrish
That the presentation and associated video presented by Sandy Duggan, Lead Program Delivery with respect to Growing Healthy Places: Mississauga be received.
Received

Item 10.2 Karen Westcott, Manager, Event Development
1. Revised FIFA World Cup 2026_Council Presentation March 4_FINAL V2.pdf
Karen Westcott, Manager of Event Development, presented plans to screen approximately 62 FIFA World Cup matches over 23 days, including all three Team Canada group-stage matches and the World Cup final, pending FIFA’s approval for non‑commercial public viewing rights. Ms. Westcott highlighted ongoing collaboration with Visit Mississauga on the relocation of the Jackalope event, as well as discussions with local BIAs on potential partnership opportunities. Ms. Westcott also noted that an application has been submitted for Mississauga to serve as a stop on the Canada Celebrates FIFA World Cup 2026 tour, a one-day national celebration that would bring a vibrant atmosphere to downtown Mississauga. Ms. Westcott noted that the forecasted budget is $75,000, with all costs managed within the approved 2026 Recreation and Culture budgets, along with an additional $50,000 contribution from Visit Mississauga.
In response to Councillor Dasko’s enquiry, Karen Westcott advised that staff are collaborating with the BIAs and anticipate having plans ready later this month. Victoria Clarke, Director of Tourism and CEO of Visit Mississauga, further noted that the Jackalope event will be relocated to the P3 parking area at Square One Shopping Centre.
In response to Councillor Kovac’s enquiry, Karen Westcott confirmed that concessions at Celebration Square are permitted. Ms. Westcott clarified that the agreement governing broadcast rights prohibits sponsorships, including the display of logos, or sanctioned by FIFA.
In response to Councillor Horneck’s enquiry, Karen Westcott outlined plans to animate Celebration Square with engaging cultural photo opportunities, ground decals, and potentially flags representing diverse communities.
