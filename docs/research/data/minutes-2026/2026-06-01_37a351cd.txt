RECOMMENDATION REPORT (WARD 3)
1. 1470 Williamsport Drive – Rental Housing Protection (RHP) Bylaw permit application RH 25-001 W3 - 0305-2026(1).pdf
2. Appendix 1 - Tenant Concerns and Staff Responses(1).pdf
3. Item 6.6 Staff Presentation - 1470 Williamsport Drive.pdf
Rental Housing Protection (RHP) Bylaw permit application (under Section 99.1 of the Municipal Act) to demolish an existing six storey apartment building containing 53 units to be replaced with two 12 storey apartment buildings containing 283 dwelling units
1470 Williamsport Drive, south of Williamsport and west of Havenwood Drive
Owner: 1470 Williamsport Holdings Inc.
File: RH 25-001 W3
Brandon Williams, Planner, provided an overview of the application, the Rental Housing Protection By-law requirements, tenant assistance measures, timelines, and next steps.
Greg Goring, Resident, inquired about the allocation of replacement units and how priority would be determined. Michi McCloskey,Senior Associate, Sajecki Planning Inc., advised that units would be offered on a priority basis, notice would be provided to tenants at least six months in advance, relocation assistance would be available, and accessibility needs would be considered.
Ramsha Haq, Resident, inquired about the affordability of replacement units and tenant return. B. Williams advised that replacement rent would be based on the last rent paid before tenants vacated the building.
Councillor Fonseca thanked residents and tenants for their feedback and the applicant for working with staff and residents throughout the process. Mayor Parrish thanked Councillor Fonseca for her efforts.
RECOMMENDATION PDC-0032-2026
Moved By Councillor C. Fonseca
That the report dated May 13, 2026, from the Commissioner of Planning and Building, 1470 Williamsport Drive – Rental Housing Protection (RHP) Bylaw permit application RH 25-001 W3 be approved in accordance with the following:
That City Council approve the RHP Application under Section 99.1 of the Municipal Act to permit the demolition of fifty-three (53) rental dwelling units at 1470 Williamsport Drive, subject to the following conditions:
The owner shall provide tenant relocation and assistance to all Eligible Tenants of the fifty-three (53) existing rental dwelling units proposed to be demolished at 1470 Williamsport Drive, including the right to return to a rental dwelling unit of a similar unit type and at a similar rent, all to the satisfaction of the Commissioner, Planning and Building Department; and
The owner shall enter into, and register on title at 1470 Williamsport Drive , an agreement pursuant to Section 99.1 of the Municipal Act, 2001, to the satisfaction of the City Solicitor and the Commissioner, Planning and Building Department.
That City Council authorize the Commissioner, Planning and Building Department to issue Preliminary Approval of the application under Section 99.1 of the Municipal Act for the demolition of the fifty-three (53) existing rental dwelling units at 1470 Williamsport Drive after all of the following have occurred:
The conditions in Recommendation 1 above have been fully satisfied;
The Zoning By-law Amendment has come into full force and effect;
The issuance of the Notice of Approval Conditions for site plan approval;
The execution and registration of agreements pursuant to Section 99.1 of the Municipal Act, 2001, securing the condition in Recommendation 1a above; and
The City has received confirmation that all fifty-three (53) of the existing rental dwelling units proposed to be demolished are vacant.
That City Council authorize the Chief Building Official to issue a Demolition Control permit under Section 33 of the Planning Act after the Commissioner, Planning and Building Department has issued the Preliminary Approval referred to in Recommendation 2 above.
That City Council authorize the Chief Building Official and Director, Building Division to issue a residential demolition permit for 1470 Williamsport Drive after the Commissioner, Planning and Building Department has issued the Preliminary Approval referred to in Recommendation 2 above, which may be included in the Demolition Control permit on condition that:
The owner erects the proposed new rental building no later than four (4) years from the date that the demolition of such building commences; and
Should the owner fail to complete the proposed new rental building within the time specified in Recommendation 4(c) above, the City Clerk shall be entitled to enter on the collector’s roll, as with municipal property taxes, an amount equal to the sum of twenty thousand dollars ($20,000.00) per dwelling unit for which a demolition permit is issued, and that such amount shall, until payment, be a lien or charge upon the land for which the demolition permit is issued.
That City Council authorize the appropriate City officials to take such actions as are necessary to implement City Council's decision, including execution of the Section 99.1 agreement and other related agreements.
That 2 (two) oral submissions be received.
YES (11)
Councillor S. Dasko, Councillor C. Fonseca, Councillor J. Kovac, Councillor D. Damerla, Councillor M. Mahoney, Councillor S. McFadden, Councillor A. Tedjo, Councillor J. Horneck, Councillor M. Reid, Councillor B. Butt, and Councillor N. Hart
Carried (11 to 0)

6. Item 6.8 Staff Presentation - LA.07.CIT.pdf
Proposed Revisions to Pre-Zoning of Protected Major Transit Station Areas (MTSAs)
File: LA.07-CIT
Tim Lee, Planner, provided an overview of the proposed revisions to the pre-zoning of Protected Major Transit Station Areas, including growth along transit corridors, the Mayor’s Task Force Report, updates to the proposed Zoning By-law Amendment, the proposed Hospital Growth Centre, and related recommendations.
Shawn Kerr, Vice President of Community Development and Public Affairs at Trillium Health Partners, spoke to the importance of protecting the hospital flight path while supporting city-building objectives and working collaboratively with planning staff and landowners.
Kevin Freeman, Director, Planning and Building, Kaneff Properties, spoke to building height permissions, flight path considerations, and the proposed implementing zoning provisions.
Committee Members raised questions regarding compatibility with the hospital flight path, the appropriateness of the Committee of Adjustment route, and whether a holding provision or satisfaction letter process would be sufficient. Planning staff provided comments regarding the unique nature of the planning framework, the proposed review process, and the need to ensure there are no conflicts with future heliport operations.
