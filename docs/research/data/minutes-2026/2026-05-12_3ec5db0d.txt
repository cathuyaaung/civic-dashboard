7.1
Matthew Sweet, Manager, Active Transportation regarding the Cycling Master Plan Update
M. Sweet spoke to the Cycling Master Plan (CMP) update, providing background information and highlighting the transportation equity lens. M. Sweet further spoke to the CMP plan, highlighting the following:
Goals and recommendations
Cycling network prioritization and project types (integrated cycling infill, and quick builds)
Impact of Bill 212 and Bill 60 on Quick Build Projects
5-year plan project plan with facility types, and
Financial impact of ultimate builds and quick builds.
M. Sweet noted that the final CMP document would be presented to General Committee in June and circulated to Committee once finalized.
Committee Members commented and inquired about the following:
Differences between cost figures related to the 5-year plan and quick build projects
Alternate methods to deliver projects removed from the quick build program
Coordination with neighbouring municipalities to avoid abrupt network terminations at boundaries
Timeline for presenting the CMP update to Council
Whether the long-term timeline changed from the 2018 CMP
Hurontario LRT project and earlier delivery of cycling infrastructure
Targets and metrics for promotion and education initiatives
M. Sweet responded to questions providing information on cost estimates, alternative delivery approaches and quick build projects, network connectivity across municipalities, and CMP completion timelines.
RECOMMENDATION MCAC-0009-2026
Moved By Bill Johnston
That the deputation and associated presentation from Matthew Sweet, Manager, Active Transportation regarding the Cycling Master Plan Update, be received.
Received
7.2
Item 7.1 Rahul Mehta, Resident
R. Mehta inquired when the 2025 Cycling Master Plan (CMP) update will be available to the public and spoke to equity and consistency considerations related to the continuity and quality of cycling infrastructure, network gaps, transitions, and user comfort. R. Mehta inquired about long-term network timelines, delivery pace, and treatment of projects outside the 5-year plan; and further asked about clarity on infrastructure status, funding references, and the distinction between planning studies and construction.
In response, Matthew Sweet, Manager, Active Transportation spoke to quick builds and accelerating the CMP timeline, costs reflecting infrastructure value estimates, progress reporting, hydro corridor trails, and recent funding announcements.
RECOMMENDATION MCAC-0010-2026
Moved By Moaz Ahmad
That the verbal deputation from Rahul Mehta, Resident regarding the Cycling Master Plan Update, be received.
