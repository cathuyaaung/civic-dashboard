MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED
10.1
Max Gill, Manager Traffic Services & Road Safety regarding an update on the second phase of The Provincial Road Safety Initiatives Funding (verbal)
M. Gill provided an update on the Province’s Road Safety Initiative funding and noted that the initial $2.2 million would support traffic calming measures in school zones, including signage, pavement markings, and beacons. He further outlined that the City
might be eligible for up to $8.9 million in additional funding through a reimbursementbased program and that a report would be brought to a future General Committee and that the report would be shared with relevant committees, including the Mississauga School Traffic Safety Action Committee.
In response to a question from Councillor McFadden, M. Gill confirmed that the initial $2.2 million would continue to be allocated on a ward-specific basis in consultation with Ward Councillors.
RECOMMENDATION RSC-0017-2026
Moved By Councillor S. McFadden
That the verbal update by Max Gill, Manager Traffic Services & Road Safety regarding the second phase of The Provincial Road Safety Initiatives Funding be received
Received
10.2
Councillor Dasko regarding a motion entitled Blinded by the Light: Improving Public Safety by Addressing Headlight Glare
1. Blinded by the Light Motion .pdf
Ibrahim Naqi, Citizen Member, inquired about the feasibility of an enforcement-led public awareness campaign. Officer Watts, Ontario Provincial Police advised that Project Erase is the closest existing initiative that may address concerns related to headlights and suggested referring the inquiry to Constable D'Amico, Peel Regional Police for further information. The committee engaged in a discussion with the following points:
Federal consultation conducted from March 6 to April 20, 2026.
Consultation survey results were circulated to the Road Safety Committee.
Concerns raised regarding older vehicles operating with high beams activated.
Noted that vehicle headlights are sometimes modified without awareness of appropriate standards.
RECOMMENDATION RSC-0018-2026
Moved By Sunil Sharma
That the motion entitled Blinded by the Light: Improving Public Safety by Addressing Headlight Glare brought forward by Councillor Dasko be received.
Received

Citizen Member Updates
