15.1
A Notice of Motion regarding Movie Nights Across Mississauga’s Parks – Bring Neighbourhoods Together (Councillor Horneck)
1. Movie Nights Across Mississauga Proposal 2026 - Revised Version w Signature 3.pdf
Members of Council expressed support for the Notice of Motion, noting its potential to bring the community together. Discussion included opportunities to implement the initiative this summer, scheduling considerations to avoid conflicts, and the use of smaller active community parks.
Nadia Paladino, Director, Parks, Forestry and Environment, responded, advising that staff would review what could be implemented this year.
RESOLUTION 0092-2026
Moved By Councillor J. Horneck
Seconded By Councillor S. Dasko
Whereas: Mississauga has long had successful community movie nights at Port Credit, Celebration Square and Streetsville;
And Whereas: This initiative looks to bring animation to local parks that often would not get city programing;
And Whereas The goal is to build memorable shared experiences for people especially kids and help build positive communities;
And Whereas: Participating Ward Councillors would be encouraged to add to the experience providing Pre-Show entertainment, snacks, contacting groups that may want setup booths for the public and any additional advertising to local areas;
Therefore Be It Resolved: that Parks staff procure services for summer 2027 to look for a company that could provide the needed projection, sound and licensing necessary to have movie nights in local parks in conjunction with Councillor’s offices with a goal of obtaining more favorable terms than single-event sourcing;
Be it further resolved that staff will support the events by: identifying multiple appropriate parks within each Ward for hosting the events; arranging for supplemental picnic tables, waste, recycling receptacles, and appropriate parks staffing to be available as needed during each event; arranging for a Curbex sign to advertise the event, each at no additional cost.
