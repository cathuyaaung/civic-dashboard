DEPUTATIONS
6.1
Sean Robertson-Palmer, Community Development Coordinator
1. 2026 Grants Council Presentation - Sean Robertson Palmer.pdf
Sean Robertson-Palmer, Community Development Coordinator, provided an overview of the 2026 Culture and Community Grant Program highlighting the program’s purpose, the amount of community and arts and culture grants awarded; total Investment in 2026 grants; application support for groups; groups recommended for multi-year culture grants and eligible applications recommended for the 2026 culture projects and community grants.
In response to Councillor Tedjo's enquiry regarding the funding to the Trillium Health Partners, Sean Robertson-Palmer, Community Development Coordinator explained what the initiative is. Mr. Robertson-Palmer noted that appendix 5 of the corporate report provides a summary of the discussion from the peer assessors, an evaluation summary, and further noted that the group is eligible based on current guidelines and was approved for funding based on its merit.
Councillor Tedjo raised concern with respect to why some public institutions are eligible and others are not and whether it is due to funding from other sources, Councillor Tedjo requested a review of why hospitals would be included or excluded from the application process for future years. Mr. Robertson-Palmer explained that each year's grant guidelines are reviewed and would be happy to take direction to review the eligibility criteria for 2027 applications.
In response to Councillor Kovac's enquiry, Sean Robertson-Palmer, Community Development Coordinator, explained the number of groups that were ineligible for the multi-year grant.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0062-2026
Moved By Councillor A. Tedjo
That the deputation and associated presentation by Sean Robertson-Palmer, Community Development Coordinator, with respect to the 2026 Culture and Community Grant Programs, be received.
Received
6.2
Carmen Ford, Manager, Creative Industries and Corey Poole, Music Sector Development Coordinator
1. Feb 4_Live Music Grant Pilot Presentation_GC Corporate Grants 2pm.pdf
Carmen Ford, Manager, Creative Industries and Corey Poole, Music Sector Development Coordinator, provided an overview of the Live Music Grant Pilot Program, highlighting the launch date, who administered the program, the purpose of the program, the results of the pilot, and recommendations including making the pilot program permanent and the financial impact.
In response to Councillor Dasko's enquiries, Corey Poole, Music Sector Development Coordinator, advised that the pilot was a great success and oversubscribed.
In response to Councillor Fonseca's enquiry, Corey Poole, Music Sector Development Coordinator explained the eligibility requirement that artists be Mississauga based and the communication strategy to connect with groups and individuals who were either successful or not, regarding next year’s application process.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0063-2026
Moved By Councillor S. Dasko
That the deputation and associated presentation by Carmen Ford, Manager, Creative Industries and Corey Poole, Music Sector Development Coordinator, with respect to the Live Music Grant Pilot, Results and Recommendation be received.
Received
6.3
Kristine Kane, Chair, Southside Shuffle Blues & Jazz Festival with respect to overview of 2025 festival and preview of 2026 festival
Kristine Kane, provided an overview of the 27th anniversary of the Southside Blues and Jazz Festival in 2025, and highlighted the performers who were showcased at the festival, the outreach of community activities and support to local charities, opportunities for individuals and students to volunteer, survey results of the tourism revenue generated and noted that they are in the process of securing sponsorships for the 2027 festival.
In response to Councillor Dasko's enquiry regarding attendance numbers and the number of paid individuals who organize the event, Kristine Kane, reported the turnout levels at various location sites and that everyone on the organizing team are volunteers and are not paid and their value to the success of the festival.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0064-2026
Moved By Councillor S. Dasko
That the deputation and associated presentation by Kristine Kane, Chair, Southside Shuffle Blues & Jazz Festival with respect to overview of 2025 festival and preview of 2026 festival be received.
Received
6.4
Ev Harris, Managing Director and Cindy Yip, Program Planning and Logistics Lead, MonstrARTity Creative Community with respect to 2025 monstrARTity Recap
1. 2026 MCC City of M Deputation Slides Final - Ev Harris.pdf
Ev Harris, Managing Director provided an overview of the successes of Bollywood’s Monster Mashup 2025 event highlighting the international and local performers; local food and market vendors, and family activity collaborators.
Cindy Yip, Program Planning and Logistics Lead, MonstrARTity provided an overview of the Monsters for Art Senior event, highlighting the free workshops in South Asian music, dance, poetry, and visual arts for seniors in multiple languages, the benefits of their workshop and the feedback from seniors.
Ev Harris, Managing Director expressed thanks to Council for their support and announced that the festival is expanding to three days.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0065-2025
Moved By Councillor N. Hart
That the deputation and associated presentation by Ev Harris, Managing Director and Cindy Yip, Program Planning and Logistics Lead, MonstrARTity Creative Community with respect to 2025 monstrARTity Recap, be received.
Received
6.5
Shan Abbasi, Executive Director and Maham Fatima, Coordinator of Learning and Digital Engagement, with respect to Volunteer MBC
1. 6.5 - Volunteer MBC S. Abbasi.pdf
Shan Abbasi, Executive Director and Maham Fatima, Coordinator of Learning and Digital Engagement, noted that Volunteer MBC inspires community change, increases volunteerism and enhances the capacity of non-profits in the community with thanks to the support from the city and provided an overview of Volunteer MBC's four service areas, results of client survey, impact and insight, referral service, volunteer recognition, community engagement, feedback from member organizations, 2025 - 2027 strategic plan, upcoming events and explained the ways Council can support the Mississauga community with the helpful resources Volunteer MBC would provide to Members of Committee.
In response to Councillor Kovac's enquiry, Shan Abbasi, Executive Director advised that one of the key areas that the program focuses on is social isolation and vulnerable seniors.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0066-2026
Moved By Councillor C. Fonseca
That the deputation and associated presentation by Shan Abbasi, Executive Director and Maham Fatima, Coordinator of Learning and Digital Engagement,with respect to Volunteer MBC, be received.
Received

9.1
2026 Culture and Community Grant Programs
1. 2026 Culture and Community Grant Programs - 0029-2026.pdf
2. Appendix 1- 2026 Grant Eligibility Criteria.pdf
3. Appendix 2- 2026 Peer Assessment Committees.pdf
4. Appendix 3 - 2026 Culture and Community Grants Recommendation Summary.pdf
5. Appendix 4 - 2026 Culture Projects Grant Allocation.pdf
6. Appendix 5 - 2026 Grant Evaluation Summary Sheets.pdf
In response to Councillor Dasko's enquiry, Sean Robertson-Palmer, Community Development Coordinator explained staff’s review and benchmarking of other jurisdiction’s appeal process and further explained the city's appeal process.
Councillor Dasko raised concern that there is no process in place where a group who is not eligible can question the evaluation and present their position and directed staff to conduct more benchmarking to foster a framework to allow for organizations to plead their case.
Raj Sheth, Commissioner, Community Services suggested a Commissioner level appeal process and advised that staff would review and report back before next year's program.
In response to Councillor Tedjo's enquiry, Sean Robertson-Palmer, Community Development Coordinator, explained the communication and marketing approach to encourage applicants and the percentage of registered community groups that received the community grant.
Councillor Tedjo spoke regarding the eligibility for public institutions and directed staff to review the criteria for eligible grants, especially for public institution applications.
In response to Councillor Hart's enquiry on how we are doing the peer assessment and what kind of guidance is provided and the differences between the groups, and would like to see how we can support Business Improvement Areas (BIAs) and how they are assessed, Sean Robertson-Palmer, Community Development Coordinator explained the evaluation process and peer assessments.
In response to Councillor Fonseca's enquiry, Sean Robertson-Palmer, Community Development Coordinator advised that Councillors have access to view the group registry and explained the tracking of resources which include groups at the regional level and follow up with groups for their final report.
In response to Mayor Parrish's enquiry, Sean Robertson-Palmer, Community Development Coordinator, explained the criteria applied to award the Trillium Health Partners a $10,000grant.
Mayor Parrish has no objections to Trillium Health Partners being awarded the grant to reach out to the community and agrees with Councillor Tedjo's direction to review eligibility criteria and also to do benchmarking of other jurisdictions.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0067-2026
Moved By Mayor Parrish
That the Corporate Report entitled “2026 Culture and Community Grant Programs” dated January 13, 2026, from the Commissioner of Community Services be approved.
That all necessary by-laws be enacted.

ABSENT (4)
Councillor D. Damerla, Councillor M. Mahoney, Councillor S. McFadden, and Councillor B. Butt

9.2
Live Music Grant Pilot – Results and Recommendation
1. Live Music Grant Pilot – Results and Recommendation - 0053-2026.pdf
2. Appendix 1_Live Music Grant Pilot - Grant Recipients 2023-2025.pdf
3. Appendix 2_Live Music Grant Pilot Testimonials.pdf
Councillor Fonseca directed staff to report back when the live music directory is available to show where there are gaps and opportunities.
In response to Councillor Fonseca's enquiry Corey Poole, Music Sector Development Coordinator spoke regarding the music asset study that identified core and occasion live music venues and noted that staff would be reaching out to BIAs and Councillor's offices to identify occasion space to add to the database.
Councillor Dasko raised concerns that the program is underfunded and that the amount be recommended is not enough.
Andrew Whittemore, Commissioner, Planning and Building suggested looking at the Mississauga Accommodations Tax (MAT) to fund artists, and it is just for not-for-profit artists, and will come back with a review of the music strategy for future years.
Mayor Parrish directed staff to conduct benchmarking on the amount neighbouring cities are spending on their live music programs and would like to see all grant money benchmarked in the future.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0068-2026
Moved By Councillor S. Dasko
That the report entitled “Live Music Grant Pilot – Report and Recommendation” dated February 4, 2026, be received by General Committee.
That staff be authorized to make the City of Mississauga’s Live Music Grant permanent.
YES (7)
Councillor S. Dasko, Councillor C. Fonseca, Mayor Parrish, Councillor A. Tedjo, Councillor J. Horneck, Councillor M. Reid, and Councillor N. Hart
ABSENT (4)
Councillor D. Damerla, Councillor M. Mahoney, Councillor S. McFadden, and Councillor B. Butt
