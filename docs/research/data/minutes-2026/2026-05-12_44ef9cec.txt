DEPUTATIONS
6.1
Item 9.2 - Megan Hobson, Hobson Built Heritage and Craig Goodman, CS&P Architects
1. HPA Presentation 7059 Second Line West Meadowvale (Rotherglen School).pdf
Megan Hobson, Hobson Built Heritage spoke to the proposed alteration to 7059 Second Line West and noted the following highlights:
location, setback and lot consolidation
tree preservation
traffic flow and parking
integration of the rear brick wall and flooring of the heritage home into the addition
distance of the back corner lot to adjacent residential lots; and
design elements.
Craig Goodman, CS&P Architects, spoke to 26 years of operation of Rotherglen School in the heritage district and noted it as a community asset.
RECOMMENDATION HAC-0029-2026
Moved By Vice Chair D. Cook
That the deputation and presentation by Megan Hobson, Hobson Built Heritage and Craig Goodman, CS&P Architects Inc., with respect to item 9.2 - Request to Alter a Heritage Designated Property at 7059 Second Line West (Ward 11), be received.
Received
6.2
Item 9.2 - Greg Laughton, Greg Young, David Moir and Terry Wilson on behalf of the Meadowvale Village Heritage Conservation District Review Committee
1. Meadowvale Village Heritage Conservation District Review Committee slides.pdf
Greg Laughton, David Moir, Greg Young and Terry Wilson of the Meadowvale Village Heritage Conservation District Review Committee spoke to the following concerns:
the addition maintaining the heritage characteristics of the village and following the criteria of the district plan
scale, massing and design of the addition
the location of the elevator
window orientation, materials and design
fit, size and location of the proposed addition; and
traffic flow, congestion and safety.
RECOMMENDATION HAC-0030-2026
Moved By J. Holmes
That the verbal deputation by Greg Laughton, Greg Young, David Moir and Terry Wilson of the Meadowvale Village Heritage Conservation District Review Committee with respect to item 9.2 - Request to Alter a Heritage Designated Property at 7059 Second Line West (Ward 11), be received.
Received
6.3
Item 9.2 - Justin Lam, Resident
1. Opposition to Rotherglen Expansion.pdf
Justin Lam, Resident spoke to the following concerns:
privacy concerns with the height of the addition
construction damage to adjacent properties
parking issues and traffic
blocking of sunlight on adjacent residential lots due to the size of the addition
location of HVAC units; and
drainage issues.
Committee members noted that the site plan process would address some of the concerns outlined.
RECOMMENDATION HAC-0031-2026
Moved By M. Wilkinson
That the verbal deputation and presentation by Justin Lam, Resident with respect to item 9.2 - Request to Alter a Heritage Designated Property at 7059 Second Line West (Ward 11), be received.
Received
6.4
Item 9.2 - Barry Coutts, Resident
1. B. Coutts Email dated May 8 2026.pdf
2. Rotherglen-School 1 way 2nd LW- Old Derry Rd .pdf
Barry Coutts, Resident spoke to the following concerns:
traffic congestion
potential need for a sidewalk on the east or west side of Second Line West and a crosswalk; and
review access for vehicles with a potential separate entrance off Second Line West.
Committee members noted that an entrance off Second Line West would have a negative impact on the Village and that site plan approval would address issues with traffic flow.
RECOMMENDATION HAC-0032-2026
Moved By J. Holmes
That the deputation and presentation by Barry Coutts, Resident with respect to item 9.2 - Request to Alter a Heritage Designated Property at 7059 Second Line West (Ward 11), be received.
Received

Committee members spoke to the application noting that it felt incomplete and noted that more effort would be required to mitigate the impact of the addition proposed.
