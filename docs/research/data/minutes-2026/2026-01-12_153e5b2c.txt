7.
DEPUTATIONS
Considered on January 12:
7.1
Andrew Grantham, Executive Director and Senior Economist, CIBC Capital Markets regarding the Economic Outlook
A. Grantham provided an overview on Ontario and overall the Canadian Economy highlighting the US economy, tariff exemptions, immigration, US GDP drivers, Canadian inflation, employment, interest rates, and recovery outlook.
Councillor Horneck inquired whether Canada shows a US-style K-shaped economy and asked about housing market trends in Ontario. In response, A. Grantham provided information on trends in the Canadian economy and spoke to the housing economy in Ontario.
Mayor Parrish spoke to and inquired about shifts in condo investments and purpose-built rentals. In response, A. Grantham spoke to factors impacting condo market shifts and future outlook.
RECOMMENDATION BC-0001-2026
Moved By Councillor J. Horneck
That the deputation and associated presentation from Andrew Grantham, Executive Director and Senior Economist, CIBC Capital Markets with respect to the Economic Outlook, be received.
Received
7.2
Christina Kakaflikas, Director, Economic Development regarding Mississauga’s Economic Update
C. Kakaflikas spoke to local economic indicators related to business growth, labour market; business investment trends, sources and industries; factors impacting business outlook and projected trends.
Committee Members engaged in discussion, commenting and inquiring about the following:
Trends on U.S market impact on companies in GTA/Mississauga
Responsiveness to international inquiries/business attraction marketing
Creative industries, retail, placemaking, City Centre plans
Underfunding of professional creative industries
Business outlook projection
In response, C. Kakaflikas spoke to international inquiries, collaboration with partners, campaign and engagement strategies, CRM tracking, Creative Industries collaboration, and spoke to the data on local business anticipation.
Andrew Whittemore, Commissioner, Planning & Building spoke to the underfunding of professional creative industries and plan to collaborate with creative industries for future budgets.
RECOMMENDATION BC-0002-2026
Moved By Councillor J. Kovac
That the deputation and associated presentation from Christina Kakaflikas, Director, Economic Development with respect to Mississauga’s Economic Update, be received.
Received
7.3
Item 10.2 Robert Trewartha, Director, Strategic Communications and Initiatives and Sarah Lam, Senior Research Director, Forum Research, Inc.
R. Trewartha spoke to the 2025 Resident Engagement Survey and introduced Sarah Lam from Forum Research, Inc. to present the survey results.
S. Lam spoke to the 2025 Resident Experience Survey Results, highlighting methodology, criteria, and dates of survey, including summarizing results, and drivers related to the overall resident satisfaction with City services.
Committee Members engaged in discussion, commenting and inquiring about the following:
Sample size and comparisons with previous surveys
Transit ridership
Underrepresentation of population
Results weighted by Wards
Survey questions related to transit
City services benchmarking
User fees
Survey methodology
Cost of survey
In response, S. Lam, R. Trewartha and Geoff Wright, CAO and City Manager, responded with information related to methodology, cost, and challenges of the survey.
RECOMMENDATION BC-0003-2026
Moved By Councillor D. Damerla
That the deputation and associated presentation from Sarah Lam, Senior Research Director, Forum Research, Inc. with respect to the 2025 Resident Experience Survey Results, be received.
Received
7.4
Item 10.4 and item 10.5 Faraz Agha, Director, Finance and Deputy Treasurer
F. Agha provided an overview of the 2026 proposed budget, highlighting the following:
Strong Mayor Powers legislation and timelines
Business plan priorities
Budget education campaign
Overview of revenue and expenses
Property tax dollars breakdown by City, Region and Province
Regional roads transition
Reducing budget impact
Operating budget efficiencies and cost savings
Impacts from levy reduction by service area
Overview of select capital projects
2026 budget and tax bill impacts
Deferral of projects in future years, and
Long-range financial planning strategy overview
Committee Members commented and inquired about the following:
Assessment growth percentage
Budget approval timelines
Labour costs
PILT cap and revenue impacts
Messaging related to assessment numbers
Reserve amounts
In response, F. Agha and Marisa Chiu, Commissioner, Corporate Services and Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer provided information on the assessment growth figures, budget approval timelines, costs and components related to labour and salary increases, and PILT projections.
Councillor Tedjo requested that Finance staff provide year-over-year revenue comparisons, noting decreases, and factors impacting projections that were off.
RECOMMENDATION BC-0004-2026
Moved By Councillor A. Tedjo
That the deputation and associated presentation from Faraz Agha, Director, Finance and Deputy Treasurer with respect to the 2026–2029 Business Plan and 2026 Budget and Peter Gilgan Mississauga Hospital Municipal Contribution, and Long Range Financial Planning Strategy, be received.
Received
8.
PUBLIC QUESTION PERIOD - 15 Minute Limit
Considered on January 12:
Chris Mackie, Resident asked questions regarding the following:
Union labour adjustment calculations
Highest individual salary increase
Hospital levy amounts over 4 years and inclusion in debt forecast
Assessment growth credit amount compared to previous year
Use of reserves and reserve funds
Staffing changes (Regulatory Services and Roads)
Total cost of the Huron Park Community Centre including landscaping
In response, Faraz Agha, Director, Finance and Deputy Treasurer provided information on the components of union labour adjustments, average projected salary increase, hospital levy amount, confirmed the assessment growth percentage as actual, and spoke to the reserve transfer and balance; and also provided information on FTEs related to Regulatory Services and Roads. With respect to the Huron Park Community Centre, Anna Cascioli, Director, Facilities & Property Management provided information on construction costs and lifecycle replacements.

Service Area Presentations (10 minutes per presentation)
Considered on January 12:
10.1.1
Parks, Forestry & Environment
Presented by Nadia Paladino, Director, Parks & Forestry & Environment
Committee Members commented and inquired about the following:
Breakdown of labour cost increases
Washrooms in parks
Indigenous commemoration at Grand Highlands
Status of property agreement at Sugar Maple Forest
Park acquisition program

In response, N. Paladino, Raj Sheth, Commissioner, Community Services, Sarah Rodgers, Manager, Business Planning and Support Services, Sangita Manandhar, Manager, Parks and Culture Planning, and Stefan Szczepanski, Manager, Parks Development provided information and additional details.
10.1.2
Mississauga Library
Presented by Rona O'Banion, Director, Library
Committee Members inquired about increases to in-person library visits, investment priorities with additional funds, capital budget breakdown for the Cooksville Community Centre and Mississauga Valley Community Centre, provincial gas tax use, and makerspaces.
In response, R. O’Banion spoke to factors impacting in-person library visits, investment in staffing, and makerspaces; Raj Sheth, Commissioner, Community Services spoke to the budget breakdown for the Cooksville Community Centre and Mississauga Valley Community Centre, and Marisa Chiu, Commissioner, Corporate Services, Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer spoke to the provincial gas tax.
10.1.3
Recreation & Culture (Item 10.3)
Presented by Lisa Boyce-Gonsalves, Director, Recreation & Culture
Committee Members commented and inquired about the following:
Decrease in recreation visits
Program fill rate
Outdoor pool free admission
Inclusion services support program
High demand morning programming for seniors at River Grove Community Centre
Increase in revenue and sustaining flat expenditures
L. Boyce-Gonsalves responded to questions and noted they would share the over and under-subscribed programs with Councillor Damerla.
10.1.4
Facilities and Property Management
Considered on January 13:
Presented by: Anna Cascioli, Director, Facilities & Property Management
Councillor Horneck inquired about the projected rental concessions revenue drop and A. Cascioli responded noting that the decrease is due to adjustments in budgeting and accounting and not a decrease in rentals.
10.1.5
Fire & Emergency Services
Presented by Chief Stephane Malo
Committee Members commented and inquired about the following:
Public education and concerns regarding repeat false alarms in high rise buildings
Response time standard
Budget requests regarding district chiefs
Integrating provincial legislation regarding carbon monoxide detectors
Fire station exit relocation on Queensway/Hurontario St.
Response times despite delaying building stations
Station refurbishing
Lithium ion battery fire education
S. Malo provided responses to questions and spoke to the current strategy in public education, response compliance, campaigns on carbon monoxide, station relocation exit, public reassurance regarding service despite delays in building stations, and lithium battery handling.
10.1.6
Regulatory Services
This item was considered on January 12.
Presented by Georgios Fthenos, Director, Enforcement
Committee Members commented and inquired about the following:
Revenues, expenses, and revenue-neutral FTE request
Shelter save rates
Decline in security response rate
Practices related to put-downs and live-releases
Public education and communications resources
Nuisance gatherings and housing enforcement
G. Fthenos addressed the questions raised and provided information on revenues and expenses, service levels, animal services, security response, public education, and links between enforcement and housing-related issues.
10.1.7
Transit
Presented by Maureen Cosyn Heath, Director, Transit
Committee Members commented and inquired about the following:

Future impact to ridership
Process for maximizing and optimizing the Transit Way
Provincial funding
Bus shelters
Rider surveys
Lifecycle of buses and disposal

10.1.8
Roads
Presented by Helen Noehammer, Director, Works Operations and Maintenance
Committee Members commented and inquired about the following:
Provincial funding regarding street safety plans
Street lighting program and funding request for FTEs
High frequency of replacement lights
Active Transportation FTEs
Parking Master Plan implementation and App status
Road rehabilitation actuals and forecast
Winter maintenance program annualization
Regional roads; regional roads and tax levy
Funding for noise attenuation and retaining walls

McLaughlin Trail status
Light signalization enhancement
Outfitting the traffic system management
Colin Patterson, Director, Traffic Management & Municipal Parking, H. Noehammer and Emma Calvert, Director, Infrastructure Planning & Engineering Services, and Marisa Chiu, Commissioner, Corporate Services, Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer responded with information.
