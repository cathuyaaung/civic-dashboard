6.1
Councillor Tedjo to present the following awards on behalf of the Mississauga Cycling Advisory Committee:
1. 2025 Phil Green Recognition Award - Rahul Mehta, Resident
2. Youth/School Cycling Recognition Award - Lucas Caravaggio, Resident
3. 2025 Cycling Equity, Diversity and Inclusion Recognition Award - Kevin Saldanha, Resident
Councillors Tedjo and Fonseca presented and spoke to the following awards: the 2025 Phil Green Recognition Award, the Youth/School Cycling Recognition Award and the 2025 Cycling Equity, Diversity and Inclusion Recognition Awards.
Rahul Mehta spoke to the Phil Green Recognition award and to the Peel Multicultural Council. Phil Green spoke to the cycling and active transportation in the City of Mississauga and to recognizing the cycling community.
6.2
Eleanor McMahon, Principal, McMahon Public Affairs & Communications to present the City of Mississauga with a Silver Bicycle Friendly Community Award by the Share the Road Cycling Coalition
E. McMahon presented the City of Mississauga with a Silver Bicycle Friendly Community Award on behalf of the Share the Road Cycling Coalition. E. McMahon spoke to the progress of cycling initiatives within the City, acknowledging the efforts of Members of Council and residents in promoting active transportation and cycling.
Members of Council expressed appreciation to E. McMahon for establishing the Share the Road Cycling Coalition. Discussion ensued regarding opportunities for the City to advance to Gold and Platinum designation, including enhancing access to safe active transportation infrastructure for youth and expanding the City’s cycling tourism strategy.
E. McMahon responded to questions from Members of Council and spoke to the importance of fostering a cycling culture among youth, as well as the establishment of national standards to support the development of cycling skills.
6.3
Councillor Dasko to present the Jim Tovey Lakeview Conservation Area - Birth of A New Landscape
Councillor Dasko presented “Jim Tovey Lakeview Conservation Area – Birth of a New Landscape.”
Members of Council spoke to the video tribute, acknowledging the significance of the Jim Tovey Lakeview Conservation Area and the lasting legacy of Jim Tovey. Mayor Parrish requested that staff make the video available at libraries to enhance public access and awareness.
7.
DEPUTATIONS
7.1
Eric Gervais, President of Carpell Surfaces regarding an award for a sole source turf installer for the Sports Field Artificial Turf Replacements PRC005315
E. Gervais spoke to the invitation-only public tender for Sports Field Artificial Turf Replacements, including a product offering by Carpell Surfaces, and requested that the City reconsider its decision related to the tender.
Councillors Damerla and Horneck inquired about the invitation-only procurement process, whether Carpell Surfaces had submitted a bid, the available appeals process, and potential options to reduce costs. E. Gervais responded to questions and provided further information regarding the product offering.
Eleonora Filippone, Legal Counsel Municipal Law, Nadia Paladino, Director, Parks, Forestry and Environment, and Graham Walsh, City Solicitor and Commissioner, Legislative Services, responded to questions regarding the procurement process. Staff noted that a formal bid protest process is available, outlined which proponents had submitted bids, and confirmed that the contract had not yet been awarded.
Staff further advised that they would review appeal practices used by other municipalities, including the City of Toronto’s Auditor General process, and report back to Council on the City’s bidding and appeal processes.

15.1
A Notice Motion to Waive Launch Fees – Port Credit Yacht Club (Councillor Dasko)
1. Motion to Waive Launch Fees Final.pdf
Councillors Dasko and Damerla spoke to the Notice of Motion and to waiving the launch fees for the Port Credit Yacht Club.
RESOLUTION 0110-2026
Moved By Councillor S. Dasko
Seconded By Councillor D. Damerla
WHEREAS Port Credit Yacht Club was Founded in 1936, and has been a cornerstone of sailing and waterfront life in Mississauga for nearly a century;
AND WHEREAS what began as a small group of passionate sailors has grown into a vibrant, multi-generational Mississauga club known for its strong community, active programs, and deep connection to the lake;
AND WHERESAS PCYC’s traditions are shaped not just by racing and cruising, but by the friendships, mentorship, and shared experiences that keep members coming back year after year AND participating in regattas, from youth development events to major offshore and international competitions;
AND WHEREAS for decades, sailors from across Canada and beyond have come to Port Credit to compete, learn, and connect. Events like the Steerers’ Trophy, Four Sisters Regatta, Susan Hood Trophy Race, and Lake Ontario 300 have helped shape the club’s identity as a leader in race management and on-the-water excellence;
AND WHEREAS fees for using the boat ramps have recently been implemented by the City;
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED recognizing Port Credit Yacht Club as a long standing partner, having a reciprocal storage relationship and is an affiliated group with the City of Mississauga as well, being an Ambassador to our tourism initiatives and keeping with the receptacle relationship with City of Mississauga, the PCYC be exempt of any of the costs related to the launch fees bylaw which was recently approved by City Council effective May 1, 2026.
