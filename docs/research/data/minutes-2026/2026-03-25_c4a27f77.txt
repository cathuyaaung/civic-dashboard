INDIGENOUS LAND STATEMENT
“We acknowledge the lands which constitute the present-day City of Mississauga as being part of the Treaty and Traditional Territory of the Mississaugas of the Credit First Nation, The Haudenosaunee Confederacy the Huron-Wendat and Wyandotte Nations. We recognize these peoples and their ancestors as peoples who inhabited these lands since time immemorial. The City of Mississauga is home to many global Indigenous Peoples.
As a municipality, the City of Mississauga is actively working towards reconciliation by confronting our past and our present, providing space for Indigenous peoples within their territory, to recognize and uphold their Treaty Rights and to support Indigenous Peoples. We formally recognize the Anishinaabe origins of our name and continue to make Mississauga a safe space for all Indigenous peoples.”
Miles Roque, Chair, recited the recited the Indigenous Land Statement

DEPUTATIONS
Any member of the public interested in making a deputation to an item listed on the agenda must register by calling 905-615-3200 ext.5814 or by emailing [email protected] by Tuesday March 24, 2026 by 4:00 pm
Each Deputation to Committee is limited to speaking not more than 10 minutes.
Pursuant to Section 57.1 of the Council Procedure By-law 0044-2022, as amended:
Deputations shall be received and the matter shall be referred to staff for a report, unless there is a resolution or recommendation passed to “receive” the Deputation. After a Deputation is completed, Members shall each have one opportunity to make a preamble statement and ask questions to the Deputant(s) or staff for clarification purposes only, and without debate.
7.
PUBLIC QUESTION PERIOD - 15 Minute Limit
Public Comments: Members of the Public that have a question about an item listed on the agenda may pre-register by contacting the Legislative Coordinator at least 24 hours in advance of the meeting. Following the registered speakers, if time permits, the Chair will acknowledge members of the public who wish to ask a question about an item listed on the agenda. Virtual participants must pre-register.
Pursuant to Section 58 of the Council Procedure By-law 0044-2022, as amended:
Mississauga School Traffic Safety Action Committee may grant permission to a member of the public to ask a question of Mississauga School Traffic Safety Action Committee, with the following provisions:
1. Questions may be submitted to the Clerk at least 24 hours prior to the meeting;
2. A person is limited to two (2) questions and must pertain specific item on the current agenda and the speaker will state which item the question is related to;
3. The total speaking time shall be five (5) minutes maximum, per speaker, unless extended by the Mayor or Chair; and
4 . Any response not provided at the meeting will be provided in the format of a written response.

9.5
Site Inspection Report-St.Marcellinus Secondary School (Ward 5)
1. Mavis Road and Sombrero Way.pdf
2. In Front of St Marcellnius Catholic Secondary School @ Mavis Road and Courtney Park Drive West.pdf
3. Collision Report Mavis Rd at Courtneypark Dr W.pdf
Site inspection reports for the site inspections conducted on March 10, 2026 on Mavis Road and Sombrero Way and in front of St. Marcellinus Catholic Secondary School at Mavis Road and Courtneypark Drive West for the students attending St. Marcellinus Catholic Secondary School.
RECOMMENDATION
That Peel Regional Police be requested to provide police enforcement of safe driver behavior in front of St. Marcellinus Catholic Secondary School, 730 Courtneypark Drive West, between the times of 8:00 - 8:30 AM and 2:15 - 2:45 PM.
That Parking Enforcement be requested to enforce the "no stopping" zones in front of St. Marcellinus Catholic Secondary School, 730 Courtneypark Drive West, between the times of 8:00 - 8:30 AM and 2:15 - 2:45 PM.
That Transportation and Works be requested to review the feasibility of:
installing "not a drop off lane" signage in the turn lane on Courtneypark Drive West.
adding additional "no stopping" signage on Courtneypark Drive West in front of St. Marcellinus Catholic Secondary School.
4. That the principal of St. Marcellinus Catholic Secondary School be requested to review the feasibility of locking the northwest school access door on Courtneypark Drive West next to the library to encourage parents to use the Kiss and Ride to drop off students instead of on Courtneypark Drive West.
5. That the principal of St. Marcellinus Catholic Secondary School be requested to encourage parents to use the Kiss and Ride at am school entry instead of stopping in the "no stopping" zones on Courtneypark Drive West to drop off students and to park in the school parking lot in the pm to pick up students.
6. That the Dufferin-Peel Catholic District School Board be requested to review the operation and signage, including consideration of installation of "no u-turn" signage in the Kiss and Ride at St. Marcellinus Catholic Secondary School.
7. That the Library Board be requested to ask for Parking Enforcement at the afternoon dismissal time of St. Marcellinus Catholic Secondary School, 2:15 - 3:15 pm at the parking lot in front of the library where parents stop to pick up students and cause gridlock, blocking the fire route.
The Councillor Butt advised the Committee that St.Marcellinus is located in Ward 11, not Ward 5 as indicated. The Committee engaged in a discussion and noted the following concerns:
The parking lot is underutilized and operates in an unorganized manner.
The current system is not functioning as intended.
Peel Regional Police are involved and will continue to be involved.
A re‑inspection will take place once the recommended items have been completed.
Approximately 250 collisions have been reported.
Illegal stopping continues to occur despite enforcement efforts.
Rebecca Rozario, Transportation Safety Officer for Student Transportation of Peel Region, advised the committee that the Principal of St. Marcellinus, along with City staff, will be meeting to discuss the site inspection findings. It was noted that the school community is aware of the required improvements, particularly due to increased bus traffic and parent drop‑off and pick‑up, as students attend the school from various locations
