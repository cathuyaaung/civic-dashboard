A Notice of Motion to shorten the legislated 30-day budget amendment period to 23 days, from February 4, 2026 to January 28, 2026 (Mayor Parrish)
1. Budgetmotionjan2026.pdf
Councillor Parrish spoke to the passing of the budget and that the Special Council meeting on February 4, 2026 would not be required. Councillor Horneck thanked staff for all their work on the 2026 budget.
RESOLUTION 0016-2026
Moved By Councillor J. Horneck
Seconded By Councillor N. Hart
Whereas Section 284.16 of the Municipal Act, 2001 outlines a statutory process for adoption of municipal budgets, including a 30-day budget amendment period that can be shortened by Council resolution.
Whereas the budget is deemed adopted at the end of the amendment period if there are no budget amendments approved by members of Council.
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT:
That Council shortens the legislated 30-day budget amendment period to 23 days, from February 4, 2026 to January 28, 2026.
