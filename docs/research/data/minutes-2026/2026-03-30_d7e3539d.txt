6.1
Michael Thistle, Project Prime Consultant, Landscape Architect and Jordan Wu, Project Manager, City of Mississauga with respect to Saigon Park Cricket Field and Max Ward Park Container Washroom
1. 1756-Accessibility Presentation (R1)1.pdf
Michael Thistle presented on the accessible renovations to Saigon Park and Max Ward Park and noted the following highlights:
Saigon Park would have a new cricket facility, batting cage, container washroom, intuitive pathways throughout the park and wayfinding;
universal washroom with two accessible stalls, automatic door opener, audio/emergency signals and colour contrast;
Max Ward Memorial Park would have a container washroom, barrier free walkways within the park, and accessible entry to the leash free zone;
Surface materials would consist of asphalt on walkways, textured concrete in gathering areas, the leash free zone would have a concrete entrance and wood chips in leash free zone itself; transitions will be detailed to eliminate tripping; accessible benches with back and arm support, room for wheelchairs beside benches and accessible bleachers with designated space for mobility devices;
Accessible parking spaces close to entrances; and
Lighting on all walking surfaces.
Subcommittee members spoke to the following concerns:
Drinking fountains or bottle fill stations;
Shade structures;
Bleacher railing;
Drop off zones for TransHelp;
Inclusivity in leash free zones;
Ensuring container washrooms are flush with the ground;
Location of garbage cans in washroom and coat hooks with varied heights; and
Contrasting base board to floor and walls.
In response to the concerns raised by the Subcommittee, M. Thistle and J. Wu noted the following:
Bottle fill stations would be discussed further;
That Max Ward Park has four gazebos in the park by the cricket fields;
Potential to create an overhang with the container washroom for shade;
That container washrooms would have a level transition into the washroom itself or a slight ramp for access.
RECOMMENDATION FADS-0003-2026
Moved By C. Chafe
That the deputation and presentation by Michael Thistle, Project Prime Consultant, Landscape Architect and Jordan Wu, Project Manager, City of Mississauga with respect to the accessible renovations to Saigon Park Cricket Field and Max Ward Park Container Washroom, be received.
Received
6.2
Chris Hall, Architect, The Ventin Group and Meena Nijhawan, Project Manager, Capital Design and Construction, City of Mississauga with respect to Park Washroom Upgrades at Brueckner Rhododendren Gardens and Birchwood Park
1. Brueckner and Birchwood Park Washroom Renovations.pdf
Chris Hall, Architect, The Ventin Group presented on the park washroom upgrades at Brueckner Rhododendren Gardens and Birchwood Park and provided the highlights of the accessible renovations for each park to include: the addition of a universal washroom at each location, adult change table in the universal washrooms, baby change table in single use washroom, mounting heights of sinks and insulated masonry walls.
In response, Subcommittee members spoke to garbage can location in washrooms, varied coat hook heights and colour contrasting.
In response to the Subcommittee members concerns, Meena Nijhawan, Project Manager, Capital Design and Construction noted that they would use Richard Memorial as a guideline for the accessible features.
RECOMMENDATION FADS-0004-2026
Moved By C. Chafe
That the deputation and presentation by Chris Hall, Architect, The Ventin Group and Meena Nijhawan, Project Manager, Capital Design and Construction, City of Mississauga with respect to Park Washroom Upgrades at Brueckner Rhododendren Gardens and Birchwood Park, be received.
Received
