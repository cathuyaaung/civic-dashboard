Item 10.6 Daryl Chong, President & CEO, Greater Toronto Apartment Association
Daryl Chong, President & CEO, Greater Toronto Apartment Association, spoke regarding the current Landlord Tenant Board process on renovictions and the penalties associated with landlords who operate in bad faith
Item 10.6 was forward for discussion and vote
Councillor Butt stated that renovictions are a provincial responsibility, expressed concern that the proposed by-law would duplicate provincial regulations, and advised he would not support the by-law.
Councillor Reid referenced the evictions at the privately owned property and noted the advocacy that resulted and stated that the by-law offers an opportunity for the City to help protect tenants and confirmed his support.
Mayor Parrish spoke in support of the proposed by-law and raised concerns about the province’s slow pace in addressing these issues.
Councillor Damerla stated that the by-law may not benefit tenants, could add costs, and would not address their concerns, and confirmed her opposition to the by-law.
In response to Councillor Horneck’s inquiry, D. Tudino, explained the challenges of distinguishing issues in owner‑occupied homes where the owner is also the landlord.
Graham Walsh, Commissioner of Legislative Services and City Solicitor, explained the current tribunal system.
Mayor Parrish questioned why protections appear to favour non‑compliant landlords, particularly those who do not declare rental units, avoid paying taxes, and do not obtain rental licenses.
Councillor Tedjo suggested that the program be reviewed over time and recommended directing staff to determine appropriate review timelines.
Councillor Hart stated that the proposed by-law presents an opportunity to send a clear message to non‑compliant landlords regarding unacceptable behaviour and emphasized that landlords should not take advantage of tenants. He expressed support for the by-law.
In response to Councillor Horneck's request for elaboration on the initiative, Andrew Whittemore, Commissioner of Planning and Building explained that Council had previously received a comprehensive housing report and had directed staff to explore a targeted enforcement blitz. A. Whittemore noted that staff are updating the existing by-law with an increased focus on education, as many tenants in basement apartments are unaware of their rights.
Rick Conard, Chief Building Official, advised that barriers to creating second units have been removed resulting in increased unit registrations.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0145-2026
Moved By Councillor N. Hart
That the deputation by Daryl Chong, President & CEO, Greater Toronto Apartment Association with respect to the Implementation of a Rental Repairs and Renovations Licensing By-Law to regulate renovictions (City Wide) be received.
Received
