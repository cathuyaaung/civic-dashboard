7.4
George Batsakis, Resident (Ward 4) with respect to tall grass concerns on neighbouring property
George Batsakis' daughter Tina, spoke regarding the matter and provided comments on the circumstances affecting her family with respect to tall grass concerns on neighbouring property
Councillor Kovac commented on the situation and noted that the matter is currently before the courts.
Georgios Fthenos, Director, Enforcement also addressed the Committee and provided remarks regarding the matter.
Graham Walsh, City Solicitor & Commissioner, Legislative Services advised that the issue is the subject of a Human Rights Tribunal proceeding and that efforts are ongoing to reach a resolution. Mr. Walsh noted that staff are exploring alternative options with the homeowner in an effort to resolve the matter and that this remains the City's current approach.
In response, to Mayor Parrish and Councillor Damerlas's enquiries regarding whether the City had previously undertaken this type of remediation work and questioned why it could not continue in this instance and why remediation efforts could not proceed while the matter remains before the courts, Graham Walsh, City Solicitor & Commissioner, Legislative Services advised that the City is working within the constraints of the ongoing legal proceedings and remains committed to pursuing a resolution through discussions with the homeowner and consideration of alternative solutions.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0387-2026
Moved By Councillor J. Kovac
That the deputation from George Batsakis, Resident (Ward 4) with respect to tall grass concerns on neighbouring property be received.
