7.1
Dr. Sarah McClennan , Chief of Emergency and Urgent Care & Shawn Kerr Vice-President Community Development & Public Affairs Trillium Health Partners regarding increase in injuries related to E-Scooters & Micro Mobility Devices (New Presentation Added)
1. Emergency Department Presentations Related to E-Scooters and Micro-Mobility Devices.pdf
2. Emergency Department Visits Related to E-Scooters and Micro-Mobility Devices.pdf
Dr. Sarah McClennan presented information on the increase in injuries related to e‑scooters and other micro mobility devices. Since 2023, emergency room data indicates a rise in injuries, including among individuals under the age of 18. Dr. Sarah McClennan outlined key trends over time, identified populations most affected, and described common injuries treated in emergency rooms, including fractures, sprains, cuts and lacerations, head injuries, and upper extremity trauma.
The Committee engaged in discussion and made the following comments:
The type of data collected by emergency rooms and the tracking methods used (e.g., by hospital and location).
Whether injury data identifies occurrences on local roads versus highways.
Whether the data is specific to e‑scooter-related injuries.
The importance of helmet use and the promotion of helmet safety via signs.
Dr. McClennan responded to the committees comments and noted that the emergency room notes captures incidents that have occurred.
Matthew Sweet Manager, Active Transportation and Matthew Aymar, Peel Public Health and Dr. McClennan noted that they would meet offline to discuss data collection of incidents.
RECOMMENDATION RSC-0009-2026
Moved By D. King
That the deputation and associated presentation from Dr. Sarah McClennan , Chief of Emergency and Urgent Care & Shawn Kerr Vice-President Community Development & Public Affairs Trillium Health Partners regarding increase in injuries related to E-Scooters & Micro Mobility Devices be received.
Received

Pablo Ramirez, Resident spoke to his reliance on the shared e‑scooter program and noted that a pause in the program would have a direct impact on his daily commute.
The Committee engaged in discussion with the resident regarding:
The frequency of use, noting the program operates on a seasonal basis;
Helmet use, including whether helmet used is personally owned or provided through the shared program
Preferred lane usage when operating an e‑scooter, including the use of bike lanes

Update from MCAC Representative to Road Safety Committee
Moaz Ahmed, Citizen Member, Mississauga Cycling Advisory Committee Representative advised that a group in Mississauga Adaptive and Inclusive Micromobility will host a forum on Wednesday at Carmen Corbasson Community Centre. Road Watch Statistics
1. City of Miss RW stats March 2026.pdf
Claudio D'Amico, Constable Peel Regional Police shared Road Watch statistics with the Committee and advised that the reporting process has been updated to a standalone form, allowing residents to submit complaints requesting Police Enforcement.
