DEPUTATIONS
7.1
Victoria Kramkowski, Senior Manager, Government and Community Relations, Toronto and Region Conservation Authority and Andrew Kett, Director, Corporate Services, Credit Valley Conservation regarding Conservation Authorities and programming
Melissa Williams, Senior Manager, Strategy and Government Relations, Credit Valley Conservation spoke to the provincial legislative changes impacting Conservation Authorities, and further spoke to local projects including the Jim Tovey Lakeview Conservation Area, flood forecasting, mapping, and municipal coordination activities.
V. Kramkowski provided updates on erosion management, SNAP programs, Climate Ready Homes, Partners in Project Green, and watershed planning work.
Councillor Butt stated appreciation for the work of the TRCA and CVC and expressed concern regarding provincial consolidation of conservation authorities.
RECOMMENDATION EAC-0012-2026
Moved By Councillor Butt
That the deputation and associated presentation from Victoria Kramkowski, Senior Manager, Government and Community Relations, Toronto and Region Conservation Authority and Melissa Williams, Senior Manager, Strategy and Government Relations, Credit Valley Conservation regarding Conservation Authorities and programming, be received.
Received
7.2
Alice Casselman, Association for Canadian Educational Resources (ACER) regarding ACER programming and other updates
A. Casselman spoke to ACER programming, including community workshops, emergency preparedness, and tree programs; and further highlighted hazard risk assessment, 72-hour emergency kits, community mapping exercises, youth programs, including community scientists and micro-drone pilot training.
A. Casselman referenced ACER's upcoming annual general meeting and invited participation.
Councillor Dasko expressed appreciation for ACER's work.
RECOMMENDATION EAC-0013-2026
Moved By Councillor Dasko
That the deputation and associated presentation from Alice Casselman, Association for Canadian Educational Resources (ACER) regarding ACER programming and other updates, be received.
Received
7.3
Carrah Bullock, Supervisor, Climate Policy regarding progress on the 2019 Climate Change Action Plan
C. Bullock presented an update on the 2019 Climate Change Action Plan, including corporate and community greenhouse gas emissions, outlining buildings and clean energy, low emissions mobility, resilient and green infrastructure, community engagement and partnerships, and circular economy.
Councillor Tedjo inquired about funding changes to the One Million Trees program and Dianne Zimmerman, Manager, Environment advised staff would follow-up with Forestry.
Committee Members commented and inquired about:
Hydrogen hub progress and bus deployment
Recycling changes for multi-residential developments
Waste collection transition from the Region to the City and opportunity to improve waste diversion
Biodiversity targets in the current Climate Change Action Plan
Whether 2030 targets are achievable
How ACER can support City programs
Green building standards becoming mandatory
D. Zimmerman and C. Bullock responded to questions.
Direction was provided to staff to follow up with Forestry regarding funding impacts to the One Million Trees program.
RECOMMENDATION EAC-0014-2026
Moved By Melanie Kramer
That the deputation and associated presentation from Carrah Bullock, Supervisor, Climate Policy regarding progress on the 2019 Climate Change Action Plan -2026, be received.
Received
7.4
Item 10.3, Sumeet Jhingan, Manager, Energy Management
S. Jhingan presented the annual update on the 5-Year Energy Conservation Plan.
Councillor Butt inquired about the following:
Street lighting and pathway lighting, including concerns raised by residents regarding illumination levels
Whether illumination can be improved while reducing energy use through newer technology
Overview of City approach to energy efficiency for street lighting
Solar-powered pathway and street lighting
Update on improvements in solar lighting technology in parks and multi-use trails
S. Jhingan addressed questions.
Direction was provided to staff to review solar lighting technology and report back.
A. Casselman, Community Group Representative inquired whether green roofs or blue roofs were included in the program and S. Jhingan responded.
RECOMMENDATION EAC-0015-2026
Moved By Derek Stone
That the deputation and associated presentation from Sumeet Jhingan, Manager, Energy Management regarding the 2025 Annual Energy Report, be received.
Received
7.5
Jamaal Haynes, Coordinator, Waste Management regarding the 2026 Circular Economy Initiatives Update
J. Haynes spoke to the 2025 waste diversion metrics recap, repair hub statistics and volunteer expansion, and 2026 circular economy initiatives.
Karen Pawlowski, Citizen Member inquired whether, in expanding the volunteer base, consideration has been given to targeted outreach to seniors, noting that some seniors may not access the volunteer website.
In response, J. Haynes spoke to the volunteer webpage where residents can apply.
Councillor Mahoney, Chair suggested exploring ways to engage seniors, including through community centres and senior programming.
Direction was provided to staff to review options to provide targeted outreach to seniors regarding volunteer opportunities.
Councillor Mahoney, Chair expressed support for the C Cafe reusable cup initiative.
RECOMMENDATION EAC-0016-2026
Moved By Karen Pawlowski
That the deputation and associated presentation from Jamaal Haynes, Coordinator, Waste Management regarding the 2026 Circular Economy Initiatives Update, be received.
Received
