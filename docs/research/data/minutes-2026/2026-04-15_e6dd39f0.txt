Item 10.2 Carolyn Ryall, Director, Rapid Transit Program Office (Presentation Attached)
1. 7.4 Council_Presentation_April_15_2026_Final.pdf

15.1
A Notice of Motion regarding complimentary access to MiWay for all active Regular and Reserve members of the Canadian Armed Forces and for veterans (Councillor Damerla)
1. UPDATED - Mississauga_Complimentary_MiWay_Transit_FOR_CAF_Veterans.pdf
Members of Council spoke in support of the Motion. Geoff Wright, City Manager, advised that staff are able to accommodate the request and will proceed with implementation. He further noted that Council will be notified by email of the effective date.
Councillor Damerla left the meeting at 10:55AM.
RESOLUTION 0083-2026
Moved By Councillor D. Damerla
Seconded By Councillor B. Butt
WHEREAS members of the Canadian Armed Forces (CAF), including Reservists are valued members of Canada;
AND WHEREAS many serving members, particularly Reservists, are facing increasing financial pressures due to the rising cost of living in Mississauga and the broader region, while balancing military duties, civilian employment, and family responsibilities;
AND WHEREAS members of the Canadian Armed Forces commuting from Mississauga to Moss Park Armoury or Fort York Armoury benefit from complimentary travel on MiWay as a result of the One Fare Program, while the same members commuting entirely within Mississauga to access The Toronto Scottish Regiment Armoury are required to pay the full MiWay fare;
AND WHEREAS GO Transit and the UP Express currently provide complimentary, year-round transit to eligible members of the Canadian Armed Forces and veterans across the region, demonstrating a clear precedent for transit support for military personnel;
AND WHEREAS providing complimentary MiWay transit for CAF members and veterans would be a tangible demonstration of the City of Mississauga's appreciation for their service and would align with broader regional efforts to recognize their contributions and sacrifices;
THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT:
The City of Mississauga approve the provision of complimentary access to MiWay for all active Regular and Reserve members of the Canadian Armed Forces and for veterans, upon presentation of valid military identification, CFOne card, or other proof of service as determined by the City.
The Mayor, on behalf of Council, write to the Minister of National Defence, local Canadian Armed Forces units, and veterans' organizations serving Mississauga to advise them of this initiative and to reaffirm the City's ongoing support for those who serve.
YES (10)
Councillor S. Dasko, Councillor C. Fonseca, Deputy Mayor and Councillor J. Kovac , Councillor D. Damerla, Councillor S. McFadden, Councillor A. Tedjo, Councillor J. Horneck, Councillor M. Reid, Councillor B. Butt, and Councillor N. Hart

Seconded By Councillor S. McFadden
WHEREAS Quebec Bill 21, An Act Respecting the Laicity of the State, prohibits public services from wearing religious symbols such as hijabs, turbans, yarmulkes, crosses, and other forms of personal religious expression;
AND WHEREAS Mississauga is a multicultural city, home to neighbours of many diverse faiths, religions, and cultures, and where everyone is free to practice and express their faith;
AND WHEREAS on October 2019 Mississauga City Council pass a motion opposing Quebec’s Bill 21 on the ground that it unfairly targets religious individuals wishing to express their faith;
AND WHEREAS the Province of Quebec pre-emptively used the notwithstanding clause to protect Bill 21 from legal challenges;
AND WHEREAS the Supreme Court has recently heard the case if this piece of legislation violates the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms;
THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that Mississauga City Council reaffirms its opposition to Quebec Bill 21 and continues to support building a welcoming country and city where everyone has equal opportunity to practice their faith.

A Notice of Motion to proclaim April 23rd as Spanish Language Day in the City of Mississauga (Councillor Kovac)

RESOLUTION 0085-2026
Moved By Deputy Mayor and Councillor J. Kovac
Seconded By Councillor C. Fonseca
WHEREAS, City of Mississauga proclamations are official statements by Council recognizing causes, events, and commemorations of significance that contribute to the city’s cultural diversity and public good;
AND WHEREAS, Canada Habla Español (CANHE) is a registered federal non-profit organization dedicated to promoting and preserving the Spanish language and supporting the growth and visibility of Spanish-speaking communities across Canada through cultural, educational, and community engagement initiatives;
AND WHEREAS, CANHE’s proclamation request is supported by a coalition of local Mississauga organizations, including the Canadian Latin Heritage Association, Mississauga Players, Hispanic Canadian Arts and Cultural Association, Grupo ALCE, Hispanic Women’s Network, and Latinas Leaders, all of which contribute meaningfully to the cultural, artistic, and social landscape of the city;
AND WHEREAS, April 23rd is designated by the United Nations as Spanish Language Day, recognizing the importance of multilingualism and celebrating the history, culture, and global contributions of Spanish-speaking communities;
AND WHEREAS, the City of Mississauga is home to thousands of residents who identify as Latin American, representing one of the largest Hispanic communities in Ontario, whose contributions enrich the city through vibrant businesses, arts, festivals, and community leadership;
AND WHEREAS, recognizing Spanish Language Day celebrates linguistic heritage while promoting inclusion, cross-cultural understanding, and belonging, and affirms the City of Mississauga’s commitment to a diverse, inclusive, globally connected community that values multilingualism and cultural expression;
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT:
1. The City of Mississauga hereby proclaims April 23rd as Spanish Language Day annually, in recognition of the contributions of Spanish-speaking communities and the importance of multilingualism.
2. This proclamation be recognized through appropriate civic acknowledgements.
