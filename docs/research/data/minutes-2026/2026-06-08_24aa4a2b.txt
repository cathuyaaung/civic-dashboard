DEPUTATIONS
7.1
Lisa Hirmer, Stormwater Public Artist in Residence and Rachel Pennington, Public Art Curator regarding Stormwater Public Artist in Residence June 2026 Update
L. Hirmer spoke to the Artist in Residence program, including site visits, collaboration with staff, and exploration of City waterways. L. Hirmer further spoke to public art concepts intended to raise awareness of stormwater systems, climate change, watershed connections, stormwater flow and connectivity across the watershed; and highlighted community engagement.
L. Hirmer invited ideas from Committee on community engagement.
Councillor Dasko spoke in support of the program and suggested engagement through Ward Councillors and the inclusion of local arts groups and community stakeholders.
Councillor Fonseca, Chair spoke to engaging residents who use parks, trails, and waterways.
Mike Gaudet, Citizen Member suggested incorporating historical mapping or interpretive elements to show historical water flow and context in areas such as the Jim Tovey Lakeview Conservation Area.
Councillor Dasko spoke to opportunities for alignment with existing waterfront and creek initiatives and suggested future information-sharing with conservation partners.
Councillor Fonseca, Chair noted that if Committee have additional feedback on community engagement, they can connect with the program team and Ward Councillors.
RECOMMENDATION SWAC-0016-2026
Moved By Mike Gaudet
That the deputation and associated presentation from Lisa Hirmer, Stormwater Public Artist in Residence regarding the Stormwater Public Artist in Residence June 2026 Update, be received.
Received

10.2
Stormwater Inlet and Outlet Assessment Update and Introduction of Dashboard
Councillor Dasko inquired about the rollout and communication approach for the dashboard and Chris Trombino, Acting Senior Manager, Works Administration, Operations, and Maintenance spoke to aligning information sharing with Committee meetings.
Councillor Dasko suggested updates on the inlet and outlet dashboard be included at Committee meetings.
Mike Gaudet, Citizen Member inquired if the dashboard reflects priority or regular inspections, how work orders are generated and tracked, and reasons for variance in inspection performance metrics over time.
C. Trombino addressed questions.
Councillor Fonseca, Chair inquired about incorporating Ward-level data filtering and geographic or mapping component to show downstream impacts on the inlet and outlet dashboard. In response, C. Trombino noted staff can look into adding the mapping component and report back.
M. Gaudet spoke to addressing upstream debris to reduce downstream blockages and incorporating preventative approaches alongside reactive maintenance.

12.
OTHER BUSINESS
Mike Gaudet, Citizen Member requested that additional items be added to the Committee’s Action Items List, including Toronto and Region Conservation Authority flood gauge alert follow-up information, status of communications related to the Dixie Dundas Flood Mitigation project, and status of correspondence regarding St. Edmund’s school.
In response, Scott Perry, Manager, Stormwater Assets & Programming noted the Committee’s Action Items List reflects formal direction and report-backs and noted that while some of the matters were not specifically listed in the list, a number are connected to previously addressed items or completed items.
Councillor Fonseca, Chair spoke to the ongoing work and significant activity related to the Dixie‑Dundas Flood Mitigation Project, noting that updates are available on the City’s website; and provided a status update on the St. Edmund’s school correspondence.
Direction was provided to staff to provide a high-level update on the Dixie‑Dundas Flood Mitigation Project, including how the City is communicating stormwater resilience work to the public. Councillor Fonseca, Chair noted she will work with staff to scope and structure the update for the Committee.
Direction was provided to staff to invite Toronto and Region Conservation Authority to provide an update on flood gauge information.
Paul Bunyard, Citizen Member spoke to the Subcommittee of Stormwater Advisory Committee discussions regarding flooding in the Lisgar area and the need for a better understanding of conservation area limits, creek flow, flood lines, and the interface between subdivision design and larger storm events.
P. Bunyard suggested exploring the possibility of upstream holding or retention functions in the broader area; and further suggested that Conservation Halton be invited to provide technical input related to the Lisgar area.
Direction was provided to staff to coordinate a Subcommittee meeting with Conservation Halton to examine the Lisgar-area flooding issues and technical considerations.
Mritunjay Mallik, Citizen Member requested to join the Subcommittee of Stormwater Advisory Committee and the Committee voted on the motion.
RECOMMENDATION SWAC-0020-2026
Moved By Councillor N. Hart
That Mritunjay Mallik, Citizen Member be appointed to the Subcommittee of the Stormwater Advisory Committee for the term ending in November 2026, or until a successor is appointed.
Approved
