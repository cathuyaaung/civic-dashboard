15.1
A Notice of Motion for a grant-in-lieu for Sadochok Ukrainian Nursery School (Councillor Fonseca)
1. A Notice of Motion for a grant-in-lieu for Sadochok (004).pdf
Councillors Fonseca and Dasko spoke in support of approving a grant-in-lieu for Sadochok Ukrainian Nursery School.
RESOLUTION 0124-2026
Moved By Councillor C. Fonseca
Seconded By Councillor S. Dasko
WHEREAS, Sadochok Ukrainian Nursery School Mississauga is a small, not for profit childcare centre that has operated out of St. Mary’s Dormition Ukrainian Catholic Church since 1972; and
WHEREAS, Sadochok Ukrainian Nursery School Mississauga currently provides part time (up to 6 hours per day) preschool programming for 16 children in a single classroom within St. Mary’s Church; and
WHEREAS, the 3599 Cawthra Road expansion project will convert an existing single detached house to a licensed childcare centre with a small entry addition, moving from 16 part time spaces to 47 full day spaces (two preschool classrooms and one toddler classroom) for children 18 months to 5 years old. In other words, the project will effectively create 47 new full day licensed spaces and almost triple Sadochok Ukrainian Nursery School Mississauga’s overall capacity, while adding much needed toddler spaces in Applewood and the surrounding area and allowing more parents—many of them recent newcomers—to work, study, upgrade their skills, or actively look for employment knowing their children are in a safe, licensed program; and
WHEREAS, Sadochok Ukrainian Nursery School Missississauga participate in the Canada wide Early Learning and Child Care (CWELCC) system, a federal provincial program that reduces parent fees for licensed childcare to make spaces more affordable for families. Sadochok Ukrainian Nursery School Mississauga’s CWELCC reduced parent fees are currently $297 per month, which is significantly below the true cost of care and is only possible because they operate as a not-for-profit with a volunteer board and rely on this operating funding. For the new centre, families’ monthly costs will not exceed $473 (i.e., a maximum of $22 per day under the CWELCC framework) which is significantly lower than day cares not in the CWELCC program; and
WHEREAS, Sadochok Ukrainian Nursery School Mississauga’s Parent Handbook follows a straightforward wait list policy and offers are made on a first come, first served basis as spots open up. And that recent immigrants from Ukraine, many of whom have arrived directly as a result of the war are being served at this location. And that while Sadochok Ukrainian Nursery School Mississauga does not collect income information, it is clear from addresses and daily observations (families walking or using transit, living in nearby rental buildings, etc.) that many are low income or financially constrained, and some are single parent households who rely heavily on an affordable, culturally and linguistically appropriate program for their children; and
WHEREAS, in 2026 Sadochok has been approved for a building permit to convert a single-family dwelling to a daycare, with a small addition to the building in the form of an entrance, at 3599 Cawthra Road; and
WHEREAS, affordable childcare spaces are a high priority for families in Mississauga; and
WHEREAS, for nonprofits, especially those with charitable status, development charges can be a significant financial burden — sometimes up to 20% of total capital costs — because they often lack surplus funds for capital projects; and
WHEREAS, a grant-in-lieu of DCs in the amount of $583,051.73 was provided to the Coptic Church at 124 Eglinton Avenue W, motioned by Councillor Horneck, and seconded by Councillor Tedjo on March 1, 2023; and
WHEREAS, a grant-in-lieu of DCs in the amount of $19,269.90 was provided to Eden Food for Change, motioned by Councillor Horneck, and seconded by Mayor Parrish on June 28, 2023; and
WHEREAS, the development charges associated with this building permit application, BP 3NEW 26 1271, valid to July 31, 2026, amount to $23,542.34 with the City of Mississauga share being $11,348.84 and the Region of Peel share being $12,193.50;
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT: A grant-in-lieu of development charges be provided to the applicant at 3599 Cawthra Road to be funded from the Tax Capital Reserve Fund (#33121), in the amount of $11,348.84.
YES (11)
Councillor S. Dasko, Councillor C. Fonseca, Deputy Mayor and Councillor J. Kovac , Councillor D. Damerla, Deputy Mayor and Councillor M. Mahoney, Councillor S. McFadden, Councillor A. Tedjo, Councillor J. Horneck, Councillor M. Reid, Councillor B. Butt, and Councillor N. Hart

A Notice of Motion regarding renaming Yeadon Place to Morguard Blvd (Councillor Dasko)
1. Morguard Street Renaming - June 10 2026 (004) (003).pdf
Councillor Dasko spoke to a proposed development to rename Yeadon Place to Morguard Boulevard. Mayor Parrish and Councillor Kovac inquired about the timing for bringing the related policy forward to Council.
Andrew Whittemore, Commissioner, Planning and Building, advised that the policy is anticipated to be presented to Council in the fall, with Mayor Parrish requesting that staff advance the report at an earlier date.
Councillor Damerla raised concerns regarding discussion of individuals in open session. Diana Rusnov, City Clerk, clarified that matters involving individuals would be addressed in closed session.
RESOLUTION 0125-2026
Moved By Councillor S. Dasko
Seconded By Councillor B. Butt
WHEREAS Morguard Corporation (“Morguard”) is the owner of a 3.6‑acre property located at the south‑east corner of Cawthra Road and Atwater Avenue in the City of Mississauga, on which three purpose‑built rental apartment buildings comprising 431 dwelling units are currently under construction, with occupancy anticipated in 2027;
AND WHEREAS on December 31, 2021, the City of Mississauga assigned the municipal addresses of 1205 Cawthra Road, 738 Atwater Avenue, and 1225 Yeadon Place to the buildings under construction on the subject lands;
AND WHEREAS Morguard has submitted a formal request to the City of Mississauga seeking a street name change from Yeadon Place to Morguard Boulevard, citing its long‑standing presence in Mississauga, its history of investment and development within the city, its charitable and community contributions, and the celebration of Morguard’s 50th anniversary in business;
AND WHEREAS Morguard has provided supporting documentation outlining its corporate history in Mississauga, its partnerships with the City, and its role as a long‑standing and valued member of Mississauga’s corporate community;
AND WHEREAS the subject development remains under construction with no residents currently occupying the buildings, and the proposed street name change can be implemented prior to occupancy, thereby minimizing impacts to residents, emergency services, mail delivery, and City systems;
AND WHEREAS the City of Mississauga’s Asset Naming Policy is currently suspended pursuant to previous Council direction, pending a comprehensive staff review and report back to Council respecting potential updates to the policy;
AND WHEREAS Council retains the authority to grant exceptions to City policies where it is deemed to be in the public interest to do so;
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT:
1. Council approve the renaming of Yeadon Place to Morguard Boulevard, to apply to the roadway serving the subject development, notwithstanding the City of Mississauga’s Asset Naming Policy subject to the following process;
a) The renaming of Yeadon Place to Morguard Boulevard will be posted for 30 days for public comment; and
b) City staff be directed to report to the Heritage Advisory Committee scheduled for July 21, 2026 for the committee’s review.
2. This approval be granted as a one time exception to both:
o the City of Mississauga’s current Asset Naming Policy; and
o the existing Council directed suspension of that policy;
3. Subject to review by the Heritage Advisory Committee, City staff be directed to undertake all necessary administrative actions to implement the approved street name change, including updates to municipal addressing records, mapping systems, and emergency services databases, in accordance with standard City procedures.
