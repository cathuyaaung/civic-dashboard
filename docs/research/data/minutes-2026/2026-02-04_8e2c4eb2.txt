9.
PUBLIC QUESTION PERIOD - 15 Minute Limit
Dan Anderson, Resident enquired regarding what specific residential property tax factors for the reallocation ratios apply to 2026 and are those the property tax factors for 2026 that reallocation ratios would be applied against, which would reflect an increase of 4.39% and 7.5% and not increases of only 1.61% and 3.60%.
Marisa Chiu, Commissioner, Corporate Services clarified that the Corporate Report on the agenda is for the approval for the delegation of tax authority to the City of Mississauga and that calculations of tax ratios and rates will be presented to Council in April 2026. Ms. Chiu advised that the ratios are in comparison to the range of fairness rates provided by the province; it is too premature to report on the impacts until the calculation of tax ratios are provided in April 2026; and explained the calculation of the rates.
Mr. Anderson clarified that his question is about what ratios being applied against a property tax factor and is it the same as the 2025 tax factor by the city and for the region.
Marisa Chiu, Commissioner, Corporate Services, explained the levee increase requirements, calculation of each portion based on the levee increase to calculate the global consolidated tax increase.
