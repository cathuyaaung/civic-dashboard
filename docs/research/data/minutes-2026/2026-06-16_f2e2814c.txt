Request to Demolish 1032 Barberry Lane (Ward 11)
1. Request to Demolish 1032 Barberry Lane (Ward 11).pdf
2. Appenidx 1 - Heritage Impact Assessment.pdf
Committee members spoke to the demolition and accessory dwelling unit ("ADU") design plan and inquired about the potential to have a larger set back.
Rick Mateljan, SMDA Architects Ltd. addressed the Committee members concerns and noted that the ADU design plan would not be part of the application and that the conditions requested would be taken into consideration for the application.
RECOMMENDATION MVHCD-0005-2026
Moved By G. Laughton
That the memorandum from John Dunlop, Manager, Indigenous Relations, Heritage & Culture Planning, entitled “Request to Demolish the Heritage Designated Property at 1032 Barberry Lane (Ward 11),” dated May 29, 2026, be approved with the following conditions:
1. That the side light by the main door be removed and that a more traditional window be installed;
2. That the Accessory Dwelling Unit ("ADU") is for illustrated purposes only and does not form part of the application.
Approved
