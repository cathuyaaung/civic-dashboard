RECOMMENDATION REPORT (ALL WARDS)
1. Rental Housing Protection By-law Update - 0344-2026.pdf
2. Appendix 1 - Information Report - Rental Housing Protection By-law Review - Status Update - 0116-2026.pdf
3. Appendix 2 - RHP Amending By-law 0121-2018.pdf
4. Appendix 3 - Justification Report.pdf
5. Item 6.3 - Staff Presentation - RHP By-law Update.pdf
Proposed Amendments to the Rental Housing Protection By-law 0121-2018
Sarah Elliott, Planner, provided an overview of the current Rental Housing Protection By-law, the objectives of the proposed update, the review process and findings, and the proposed amendments.
Councillor Fonseca inquired about the size of buildings captured by the proposed amendments and whether the eight per cent recommendation in the report would accommodate residents with accessibility needs. S. Elliott advised that the requirement would apply to all units and that, where tenants require accessible units, the developer would be expected to provide accessible replacement units.
Councillor Butt spoke to smaller residential buildings and requested that staff ensure opportunities for additional density continue to be considered without discouraging property owners from proposing redevelopment.
Jason Bevan, Director, City Planning Strategies, acknowledged the comments and advised that the objective is to maintain a balanced approach. J. Bevan noted that staff completed consultation, reviewed applications under the rental protection program, and consulted with colleagues in other jurisdictions.
RECOMMENDATION PDC-0037-2026
Moved By Councillor C. Fonseca
That the report dated June 22, 2026, from the Commissioner of Planning and Building titled
Proposed Amendments to Rental Housing Protection By-law be approved in accordance with the following:
That the report entitled “Proposed Amendments to the Rental Housing Protection By-law 0121-2018”, dated June 3, 2026, from the Commissioner of Planning & Building, be approved.
That an amendment to the Rental Housing Protection By-law 0121-2018, substantially in accordance with Appendix 2 be submitted to the June 24, 2026 City Council Meeting for approval.
That staff continue to monitor the efficacy of the Rental Housing Protection By-law 0121-2018 and report back to Council should further amendments be warranted.
That the Rental Housing Protection By-law Guidelines be updated to the satisfaction of the Commissioner of Planning & Building.
YES (11)
Councillor A. Tedjo, Councillor S. Dasko, Councillor C. Fonseca, Councillor J. Kovac, Councillor N. Hart, Councillor J. Horneck, Councillor D. Damerla, Councillor M. Mahoney, Councillor M. Reid, Councillor S. McFadden, and Councillor B. Butt
Carried (11 to 0)

Mississauga Official Plan 2051 (MOP 2051) – Omnibus Amendment
