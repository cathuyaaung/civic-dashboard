DEPUTATIONS
Any member of the public interested in making a deputation to an item listed on the agenda must register by calling 905-615-3200 ext. 8437 or by emailing Ani Grigoryan by Monday, May 4, 2026, before 4:00 PM.
Each Deputation to Committee is limited to speaking not more that 10 minutes.
Pursuant to Section 57.1 of the Council Procedure By-law 0044-2022, as amended:
Deputations shall be received and the matter shall be referred to staff for a report, unless there is a resolution or recommendation passed to “receive” the Deputation. After a Deputation is completed, Members shall each have one opportunity to make a preamble statement and ask questions to the Deputant(s) or staff for clarification purposes only, and without debate.
6.1
Carolyn Ryall, Director, Rapid Transit Program Office, regarding Rapid Transit Program Office Divisional Updates
1. Item 6.1 Staff Presentation - Rapid Transit Program Office.pdf
Carolyn Ryall, Director, Rapid Transit Program Office, provided an overview of Rapid Transit Program Office initiatives, including the Hazel McCallion Line (Hazel LRT), the City’s municipal role, line extension considerations, Dundas East BRT, Lakeshore BRT, timelines, budget considerations, the downtown mobility hub and transitway connection, as well as third‑party projects such as the Eglinton Crosstown West Extension and the GO Expansion Program.
Members raised questions regarding the Derry Road BRT, bridge widening, coordination with Metrolinx, bus operations during Hazel LRT construction, public consultation for future Dundas BRT segments, construction timelines, and potential extensions including loop connections. Questions were also asked regarding coordination of construction activities, impacts to Hurontario Street, and future planning for the Lakeshore BRT.
C. Ryall advised that discussions with Metrolinx are ongoing, that projects remain in the design phase, and that timelines provided are preliminary. Ms. Ryall further advised that assessments for future segments have been completed jointly with Metrolinx and that reports will be brought back to Council as projects advance. C. Ryall noted that further review would require station‑specific information to be provided through the Legislative Coordinator via email.
RECOMMENDATION TAC-0008-2026
Moved By L. Mercader
That the deputation from Carolyn Ryall, Director, Rapid Transit Program Office, regarding Rapid Transit Program Office Divisional Updates, be received for information.
(TAC-0008-2026)
Received
6.2
Maureen Cosyn Heath, Director and General Manager, Transit, regarding Update on Bill 98, Building Homes and Improving Transportation Infrastructure Act, 2026 and Canada-Ontario Partnership to Build -0202-2026
1. Item 6.2 Staff Presentation - Bill 98.pdf
Maureen Cosyn Heath, Director and General Manager, Transit, provided an overview of Bill 98, including its impacts on transit projects, service delivery, fare integration and collection, data reporting, priority routes, service integration, and specialized transit.
Committee Members raised questions regarding the use of transit data and analytics, rear‑door tap‑off requirements, costs associated with additional PRESTO devices, all‑door boarding, fare evasion, and cross‑boundary transit operations. M. Cosyn Heath advised that additional data would support planning, that a full cost analysis has not yet been completed, and that a single PRESTO device remains the most efficient approach at this time. M. Cosyn Heath further advised that the Province of Ontario will establish a framework to identify priority routes based on population and ridership.
RECOMMENDATION TAC-0009-2026
Moved By Councillor N. Hart
That the deputation from Maureen Cosyn Heath, Director and General Manager, Transit, regarding Update on Bill 98, Building Homes and Improving Transportation Infrastructure Act, 2026 and Canada-Ontario Partnership to Build - 0202-2026, be received for information.
(TAC-0009-2026)
Received
6.3
Alana Tyers, Manager, Service Development, Transit, regarding MiWay Update - Q1 2026
1. Item 6.3 Staff Presentation - MiWay Q1 Update.pdf
Alana Tyers, Manager, Service Development, Transit, provided an update on MiWay Q1 2026, including ridership trends, performance indicators, and factors impacting ridership.
Committee Members raised questions regarding service level stability, resource allocation, the UTM route extension, service hour reviews, revenue trends, and ridership patterns at neighbouring transit agencies. A. Tyers advised that there are no plans to reduce service levels and that staff are reallocating resources to address overcrowding. M. Cosyn Heath advised that ridership is being monitored to inform future service planning and emphasized the importance of encouraging transit use.

6.4
Ji-Yeon Lee, Senior Manager, Transit Service Design, regarding MiWay Fare Updates
1. Item 6.4 Staff Presentation - Fare Updates.pdf
Ji‑Yeon Lee, Senior Manager, Transit Service Design, provided an overview of MiWay fare initiatives, including the affordable transit pass and free transit for veterans and members of the Canadian Armed Forces.
Committee Members raised questions regarding eligibility and access. J.Y. Lee advised that approval is required through the Region of Peel and passes may be obtained at transit terminals once eligibility is confirmed.

6.5
Suzanne Holder, Manager, MiWay Customer Experience and Innovation, regarding MiWay's Digital Screens
1. Item 6.5 Staff Presentation - MiWay Digital Screens.pdf
Suzanne Holder, Manager, MiWay Customer Experience and Innovation, presented information regarding MiWay’s digital screen program, including current screen design, proposed enhancements, and implementation considerations.
Members raised questions regarding expansion to GO Stations, implementation timelines, display of real‑time service information, identification of express services, passenger occupancy measurement, and accommodation of mobility devices. S. Holder advised that the project will begin at identified locations, with expansion considered in future phases. A. Tyers advised that passenger counters are used to monitor bus loads and support service planning.
