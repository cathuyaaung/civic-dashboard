MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED
9.1
Proposed Heritage Designation of 47 Dundas Street West (Ward 7) CONSENT
1. Proposed Heritage Designation of 47 Dundas Street West.pdf
2. Appendix 1 CHER 47 Dundas St W.pdf
RECOMMENDATION HAC-0010-2026
Moved By Vice Chair D. Cook
That the City designate the property at 47 Dundas Street West (Ward 7) under the Ontario Heritage Act for its physical, design, historical, associative and contextual value, as per the report from the Commissioner of Planning and Building, dated January 12, 2026, and that the appropriate City officials be authorized to give effect thereto.
Approved
9.2
Proposed Heritage Designation of 500 Comanche Road (Ward 2)
1. Proposed Heritage Designation of 500 Comanche Road - 0052-2026.pdf
2. Appendix 1 500 Comanche Road Location Map.pdf
3. Appendix 2 500 Comanche CHER.pdf
4. Appendix 3 Regulation 906.pdf
5. Appendix 4 500 Comanche Designation Statement.pdf
Committee members spoke in favour of the designation and provided a history of the Shipp family contributions throughout Mississauga including Applewood Acres. Committee Members inquired if there would be photos available of the interior and exterior of the home and were advised by staff that a real estate listing would be available to view these photos.
In response to the Committee Members' inquiry, John Dunlop, Manager, Indigenous Relations, Heritage and Culture noted the history that removed consent of the Owner but spoke to communications with staff and the opportunities for the property owner to object or appeal the designation under the Ontario Heritage Act.
RECOMMENDATION HAC-0011-2026
Moved By M. Wilkinson
That the City designate the property at 500 Comanche Road (Ward 2) under the Ontario Heritage Act for its physical, design, historical, associative and contextual value, as per the report from the Commissioner of Planning and Building, dated January 19, 2026, and that the appropriate City officials be authorized to give effect thereto.
Approved
