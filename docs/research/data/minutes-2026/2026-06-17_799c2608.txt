21.1
Personal matters about an identifiable individual, including municipal or local board employees: Renaming Indian Gate Park
Public Comments:
This matter was discussed in open session
Members of Council spoke to the Notice of Motion and to the renaming of Indian Gate Park in honour of Constable Marc Pinizzotto Park who died in the line of duty on June 11, 2026.
RESOLUTION 0132-2026
Moved By Mayor C. Parrish
Seconded By Deputy Mayor and Councillor M. Mahoney
Whereas Constable Marc Pinizzotto died in the line of duty on June 11, 2026 and;
Whereas Marc Pinizzotto, a valued member of the Toronto Police Service Emergency Task Force, was with his team conducting a high-risk assignment when he was fatally shot and;
Whereas his mother Linda Pinizzotto describes him as “an incredible father, son, husband, coach and friend of many” and;
Whereas Marc Pinizzotto was an inspiration to a generation of young hockey players he and his two brothers coached over ten years and;
Whereas the Ontario Junior Hockey League released a statement saying Marc’s death was also “a loss to the entire hockey world” and;
Whereas Marc grew up in Mississauga with his two brothers and enjoyed the natural space of Indian Gate Park, which is in close proximity to the family home.
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED:
That the park currently known as Indian Gate Park be officially renamed CONSTABLE MARC PINIZZOTTO PARK with a commemorative plaque that tells his story and park benches that create a site for rest and contemplation.
YES (12)
Councillor S. Dasko, Councillor C. Fonseca, Deputy Mayor and Councillor J. Kovac , Mayor C. Parrish, Councillor D. Damerla, Deputy Mayor and Councillor M. Mahoney, Councillor S. McFadden, Councillor A. Tedjo, Councillor J. Horneck, Councillor M. Reid, Councillor B. Butt, and Councillor N. Hart
Carried (12 to 0)
