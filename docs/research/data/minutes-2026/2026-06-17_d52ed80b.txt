Site Inspection Report-St.Basil Catholic Elementary School (Ward 3)
1. ST Basil Catholic Elementary school.pdf
Site inspection report for the site inspection conducted on June 11, 2026 in front of school at 4235 Golden Orchard Drive and on school property for the students attending St.Basil Catholic Elementary School
Denna Tallia, Traffic Operations Technologist, advised the Mississauga School Traffic Safety Committee that the signage referenced in Recommendation 3 is standardized and cannot be modified. Sheelagh Duffin noted that the 30 km/h signs display different time restrictions, specifically 6:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on one side and 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. on the other, and indicated they should be consistent. D.Tallia agreed to review the matter.
RECOMMENDATION MSTSAC-0035-2026
Moved By T. Cieciura
1. That the principal of St. Basil Catholic Elementary School be requested to remind parents to:
a. stop in front of the school only where "no stopping" signs are not displayed and it is legal to stop or park.
b. if parked on the street that parents ensure that students exit vehicles on the curb side instead of into traffic.
c. when using the Kiss and Ride stop as close as possible to the end of the Kiss and Ride to allow room for more vehicles.
d. use the Kiss and Ride instead of parking on the street.
2. That the Dufferin-Peel Catholic District School Board be requested to add "Right Turn Only" signage from parking lot and "Do Not Exit" signage on entrance driveway to parking lot on south side of St. Basil Catholic Elementary School.
3. That Transportation and Works be requested to replace current tab under 30 km/hr signage with tab indicating 8 am - 4 pm, Mon - Fri, Sept 1 - June 30.
Approved
