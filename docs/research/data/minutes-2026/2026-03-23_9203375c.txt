6.1
Item 9.3 Vandana Waghela, Information Technology Auditor to present the Technology Decision-Making Framework Audit
1. AC Presentation - Tech Decision Making Audit.pdf
Vandana Waghela, Information Technology Auditor, presented an overview of the audit, including scope, objectives, key observations, decision‑making frameworks, and recommendations.
Councillor and Deputy Mayor Kovac inquired if the target timelines within the application decision‑making framework are dependent on other factors and requested clarification on the process. V. Waghela advised that IT‑level recommendations inform the enterprise framework and that similar tool requests across departments are reviewed at the enterprise level.
Councillor Horneck inquired whether earlier implementation could have avoided issues and how policy gaps are being addressed. Ryan Lim, Director of Information Technology and Chief Information Officer, highlighted technical debt reduction and noted that existing applications are assessed for modernization or removal. R. Lim added that accessibility, cybersecurity, and AI policies are currently under review.
Councillor Horneck inquired about PRESTO and requested an update on the outstanding item. Amy Truong, Director, Internal Audit, advised that the revenue component is pending confirmation of the updated agreement, including the proposed changes.
RECOMMENDATION AC-0001-2026
Moved By Councillor J. Kovac
That the deputation and associated presentation from Vandana Waghela, Information Technology Auditor, regarding the Technology Decision-Making Framework Audit, be received for information.
Received
