DEPUTATIONS
Any member of the public interested in making a deputation to an item listed on the agenda must register by calling 905-615-3200 ext. 8437 or by emailing Ani Grigoryan by Monday March 2, 2026 before 4:00 PM
Each Deputation to Committee is limited to speaking not more that 10 minutes.
Pursuant to Section 57.1 of the Council Procedure By-law 0044-2022, as amended:
Deputations shall be received and the matter shall be referred to staff for a report, unless there is a resolution or recommendation passed to “receive” the Deputation. After a Deputation is completed, Members shall each have one opportunity to make a preamble statement and ask questions to the Deputant(s) or staff for clarification purposes only, and without debate.
6.1
Christopher Trombino, Senior Manager, Works Administrative Operations and Maintenance regarding Transit Winter Maintenance
1. Item 6.1 - Transit Winter Maintenance March 3, 2026.pdf
Chris Trombino, Senior Manager, Works Administrative Operations and Maintenance, presented an overview of the Winter Maintenance Program, including service levels, transit stops, operational challenges, and salt management.
Committee Members raised concerns regarding shelter and bus stop clearing, salt usage, accessibility, bike lane maintenance, snow storage, and service standards. Questions were asked about compliance with the 24‑hour clearing standard and responsibilities near boulevards and plaza‑owned properties.
C. Trombino advised that this winter presented extraordinary challenges due to heavy snowfall, noted that several services were newly introduced this year, and confirmed staff continue to review practices, meet standards where possible, and incorporate feedback to improve service delivery.
RECOMMENDATION TAC-0005-2026
Moved By J. Giggs
That the deputation from Christopher Trombino, Senior Manager, Works Administrative Operations and Maintenance regarding Transit Winter Maintenance, be received for information.
(TAC-0005-2026)
Received
6.2
Alana Tyers, Manager, Service Development, Transit, regarding ICIP Project Updates
1. Itgem 6.2 - ICIP Project Updates March 3, 2026.pdf
Alana Tyers, Manager, Service Development, Transit, provided updates on ICIP projects, including bus shelters, accessibility improvements, signage, terminal upgrades, and next steps.
Members raised concerns regarding shelter installation delays, concrete pads at bus stops, glass partitions at GO facilities, bus lane markings, and funding programs. A. Tyers and Maureen Cosyn Heath, Director, MiWay, advised staff are working with Alectra to address delays, concrete pads remain a priority, and the Canada Transit Fund runs until 2032 with a City commitment of approximately $60 million.
RECOMMENDATION TAC-0006-2026
Moved By D. Cooper
That the deputation from Alana Tyers, Manager, Service Development, Transit, regarding ICIP Project Updates, be received for information.
(TAC-0006-2026)
Received
6.3
Suzanne Holder, Manager, MiWay Customer Experience and Innovation, regarding 2025 Report to the Community
1. Item 6.3 - 2025 Report to the Community March 3, 2026.pdf
Suzanne Holder, Manager, MiWay Customer Experience and Innovation, presented the 2025 Report to the Community, highlighting ridership trends, performance, customer satisfaction, service improvements, and priorities for 2026.
Members discussed satisfaction metrics, service reliability, cleanliness standards, construction impacts, and future service changes, particularly along the Dundas corridor and at UTM. Staff advised 2026 will focus on monitoring ridership and adjusting service levels as needed.

OTHER BUSINESS
Mark Song, Citizen Member, inquired whether the new bus signage designs comply with Metrolinx standards. A. Tyers confirmed that the designs meet accessibility requirements.
