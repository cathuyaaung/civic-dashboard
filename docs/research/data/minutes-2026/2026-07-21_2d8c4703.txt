Item 9.1 - Rachel Redshaw, Heritage Planner, The Biglieri Group with respect to 1 Godfrey's Lane
1. 1 Godfreys Lane- Heritage Committee Presentation July 13 2026.pdf
2. Opinion Letter 1 Godfrey's Lane July 13 2026.pdf
Rachel Redshaw, Heritage Planner, The Biglieri Group, provided an overview of the proposed heritage designation for 1 Godfrey's Lane (Ward 1), including the property's background, site description and history, previous cultural heritage evaluations, and an assessment of its design, historical, and contextual value. Ms. Redshaw summarized the reasons for the objection, indicating that the property does not meet the criteria for designation under Part IV of the Ontario Heritage Act.
RECOMMENDATION HAC-0052-2026
Moved By Councillor S. Dasko
That the deputation and presentation by Rachel Redshaw, Heritage Planner, The Biglieri Group with respect to 1 Godfrey's Lane, be received.
Received
6.2
Michael Scott, Michael Scott Architects with respect to Erindale Hall
1. Erindale Community Hall Small.pdf
In response to Councillor Dasko's enquiry regarding restoration of the front façade to its original appearance, Michael Scott advised that while desirable, budget constraints may limit this option.
Councillor Damerla requested information on the cost of restoring the front façade and asked staff to explore potential restoration options. Melissa Anastacio, Project Leader Senior Capital Projects, advised that the project scope had been developed through the City's asset management process and that budget estimates would be provided.
In response to Alex Hardy's enquiry regarding controlled access to the site, Mr. Scott advised that the installation of an access control system forms part of the project.
Melissa Anastacio noted that unauthorized parking by nearby condominium residents has created access challenges and advised that staff are also working to address parking impacts associated with park events.
RECOMMENDATION HAC-0053-2026
Moved By A. Hardy
That the deputation and presentation by Michael Scott, Michael Scott Architects with respect to Erindale Hall, be received.
Received

In response to Dave Cook's enquiry, John Dunlop, Manager, Indigenous Relations Culture & Heritage Planning advised that Council had previously directed staff to conduct further research and provide an objective rationale regarding the property's heritage value. Moved By Councillor S. Dasko
That the City designate the property at 1 Godfrey’s Lane (Ward 1) under the Ontario Heritage Act for its physical, design, historical, associative and contextual value, as per the report from the Commissioner of Planning and Building, dated May 22, 2026, and that the appropriate City officials be authorized to give effect thereto.
Approved

Alteration to the Part V Heritage Designated Property at 52 Front Street South (Ward 1)
1. Alteration to the Heritage Property at 52 Front Street South .pdf
2. Appendix 1 52 Front St Drawings_Redacted.pdf
3. Appendix 2 52 Front St. South - HIA Addendum - 20260413.pdf
4. Appendix 3 52 Front St S.pdf
In response to Antoine Musiol's enquiry, John Dunlop, Manager, Indigenous Relations, Culture & Heritage Planning advised that due to the property's sensitivity, a corporate report on the proposed metal fencing and lighting details will be brought to the Heritage Advisory Committee for review prior to approval. He noted that it has not yet been determined whether the matter will first be considered by the Subcommittee as they have already provided their opinion on the matter.
RECOMMENDATION HAC-0063-2026
Moved By A. Musiol
That the request to alter the Part V designated property at 52 Front Street (Ward 1) be received and the elements be considered as follows:
That the existing wooden fencing (already installed) be approved rather than the proposed wooden fencing and that further clarification be provided regarding the metal fencing component, including any proposed lighting details, and that the metal fencing and gate not be installed until the property comes into compliance;
That the proposed landscaping be approved in accordance with the zoning requirement of a minimum of forty percent;
That the board and batten siding be approved as proposed by the owner; and further,
That the proposed carport component be refused.
Approved

9.11
Request to Re-Name a Road - Yeadon Place to Morguard Boulevard
1. Morguard Boulevard Road Renaming .pdf
2. Appendix 1 - Asset Naming Procedure and Criteria.pdf
In response to Alex Hardy's enquiry regarding the heritage significance of the proposed name and how it relates to the City's naming policy, John Dunlop, Manager, Indigenous Relations, Culture & Heritage Planning, advised that there is currently no specific policy and noted that the Heritage Advisory Committee now reviews street naming requests, regardless of whether they are directly heritage related.
Councillor Dasko provided an overview of the request to name a new private road in recognition of a family associated with the property.
Dave Cook, Vice Chair expressed support for recognizing individuals and companies that have made significant contributions to the City.
In response to Lucy Zita's enquiry regarding public review, John Dunlop, Manager, Indigenous Relations, Culture & Heritage Planning advised that the proposed naming had been posted on the City's website for a 30 day public comment period.
Councillor Damerla departed at 10:19 AM
RECOMMENDATION HAC-0065-2026
Moved By Councillor S. Dasko

Approved
