8.1
Serhiy Nadal, Mayor of Ternopil, Ukraine, Nataliya Halych, President, Ukrainian Community in Mississauga and Oleh Nikolenko, Consul General of Ukraine in Toronto with respect to Mississauga–Ternopil Twinning Agreement
Oleh Nikolenko, Consul General of Ukraine, Volodymyr Palagniuk, Founder, Canada-Ukraine Twin Cities Initiative, and Nataliya Halych, President, Ukrainian Community in Mississauga, provided an overview of the twin‑city partnership initiative between Canada and Ukraine, outlining the cities that have entered into agreements with Ukrainian sister cities since 2022, and explained how the initiative could benefit the City of Mississauga and emphasized that there is no financial impact associated with participation.
Serhiy Nadal, Mayor of Ternopil, expressed thanks to Mayor Parrish and Councillors Fonseca and Dasko for their support, and provided an overview of the City of Ternopil, including its local economy.
In response to Councillor Dasko's enquiry, Serhiy Nadal, Mayor of Ternopil, and Nataliya Halych, President, Ukrainian Community in Mississauga provided clarification regarding the medical school and noted that educational elements of Ternopil would be showcased at Carassauga, with souvenirs available at the pavilion.
In response to Councillor Damerla's enquiry, Serhiy Nadal, Mayor of Ternopil, explained how the city has 10 universities with a population of 10,000.
In response to Councillor Kovac's enquiry, Volodymyr Palagniuk, Founder, Canada-Ukraine Twin Cities Initiative advised that the partnership has been in place for over 15 years and noted that, since the war, cities in Poland have been providing assistance.
In response to Councillor Kovac's enquiry, about when city twinning initiatives began, the duration of the partnership, and whether additional twin‑city relationships could be pursued, including whether Japan was aware of the initiative, Geoff Wright, City Manager, CAO advised that staff would review the matter further.
Councillor Tedjo referenced the City’s economic development policy and its criteria and questioned whether the twinning initiative should proceed through a formal assessment process. Andrew Whittemore, Commissioner, Planning and Building advised that he would take the matter back for further consideration.
Robert Trewartha explained the distinction between the City’s economic development policy and its twinning policy, noting that the twinning policy is a City policy and may require amendments to allow for additional partnerships.
Staff were directed to prepare a briefing note and circulate to Members of Council prior to the next Council meeting.
Councillor Fonseca spoke to the benefits of the partnership and asked for clarification on how the initiative would operate in practice.
Mayor Parrish expressed support for the partnership, emphasizing that it is driven by compassion rather than economic benefit.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0219-2026
Moved By Councillor C. Fonseca
That the Council of the City of Mississauga endorse the establishment of a twinning agreement between the City of Mississauga and the City of Ternopil, Ukraine.
That the Mayor be authorized to engage with the Mayor of Ternopil to formalize this partnership.
That staff be directed to take all necessary steps to facilitate the development, execution, and implementation of the twinning agreement, including the establishment of a working group with community stakeholders.
That the Twin City Program Policy (06-03-01) be updated.
YES (12)
Councillor S. Dasko, Councillor C. Fonseca, Councillor J. Kovac, Mayor Parrish, Councillor D. Damerla, Councillor M. Mahoney, Councillor S. McFadden, Councillor A.Tedjo, Councillor J. Horneck, Councillor M. Reid, Councillor B. Butt, and Councillor N. Hart

Amanda Kitson, Community Development Coordinator and Nicole Carey, Manager, Community Programs with respect to Mississauga Youth Weeks and Youth Initiatives

Nicole Carey, Manager, Community Programs provided an overview of additional youth initiatives.
In response to Councillor Tedjo's enquiry, Amanda Kitson, Community Development Coordinator, advised that there are 11,000 Saga Summer Passes available.
In response to Councillor Tedjo’s inquiry regarding the inclusion of lane swims, Nicole Carey, Manager, Community Programs, advised that lane swims are not included in the 2026 SAGA Summer Pass. 8.4
Haniya Murtaza, Chairperson, and Vaishakh Nair, Vice-Chairperson, Mississauga Youth Action Committee ("MYAC") with respect to MYAC 2025-2026 Highlights and Celebrating Youth Week
Haniya Murtaza, Chairperson, and Vaishakh Nair, Vice-Chairperson, Mississauga Youth Action Committee ("MYAC") spoke to their shared mission, ambassador team, key accomplishments, future goals, upcoming events and how to get involved.
Councillor Damerla asked how the deputants balance school responsibilities with participation on the Mississauga Youth Action Committee (MYAC). Vaishakh Nair responded that members are passionate about the work and enjoy their involvement. Councillor Damerla expressed that she was impressed with how members successfully balance school commitments while contributing to MYAC and providing opportunities for their peers to grow
In response to Councillor Reid's enquiry regarding the small business Market, Vaishakh Nair explained how the Mississauga Youth Action Committee (MYAC) helped facilitate and support the small business market event.
In response to Councillor Reid’s enquiry regarding MYAC’s connection with IDEA Mississauga, Haniya Murtaza confirmed that MYAC is connected with IDEA Mississauga and is collaborating with them on a future event.
Councillor Fonseca requested that MYAC’s newsletter be circulated to Members of Council for broader community distribution and asked whether MYAC engages with middle schools. In response, Vaishakh Nair advised that outreach to middle schools is being planned to share information about MYAC’s work, volunteer involvement, and available opportunities for students as they enter high school.
Councillor Fonseca also raised the potential for connecting MYAC with Volunteer MBC Mississauga and offered to help facilitate introductions.
Councillor Dasko asked how Council could further support MYAC ; Vaishakh Nair and Haniya Murtaza responded Council would benefit from a youth perspective, the challenge is being able to engage with youth, Councillors could promote within their community to get them more involved.
In response to Councillor Dasko’s enquiry regarding how Council could further support the Mississauga Youth Action Committee (MYAC), Vaishakh Nair and Haniya Murtaza emphasized the value of incorporating youth perspectives into decision‑making. They expressed an interest in offering feedback and insights to help ensure Council actions reflect the needs of local youth and requested Councillor’s support in promoting MYAC to address declining youth engagement.

Amendments to Open Air Burning By-law 0140-2018

Moved By Councillor D. Damerla
That the report dated March 31, 2026, from the City Manager and Chief Administrative Officer entitled “ Amendments to Open Air Burning By-law 0140-2018” be approved.
That the Open-Air Burning By-law 0140-2018 be amended to:
Define ‘Ceremonial Fire’ and permit Ceremonial Fires without the requirement for a permit issued by the Fire Chief.
Revise open air burning prohibitions to align with current provincial air quality advisory indexes; and
Define ‘City-wide Open Air Burning Restriction’ and authorize the Fire Chief or their designate to issue a City-wide Open Air Burning Restriction,with exceptions for ceremonial and cooking fire, for periods when there is an elevated risk condition, as determined by the Fire Chief or their designate.

MATTERS PERTAINING TO REGION OF PEEL COUNCIL
Mayor Parrish spoke to a motion on the Regional Council agenda addressing outstanding matters related to Peel Art Gallery, Museum and Archives (PAMA).
In response to Councillor Horneck's enquiry regarding red light camera enforcement procedures and the potential transfer of processing to the City of Brampton, Sam Rogers, Commissioner of Transportation and Works, advised that staff have no concerns with either continuing the current contracted service with the City of Toronto or moving processing to Brampton, provided there is no impact on operations or costs. He noted his understanding that the report is an update for information, not a report seeking direction.

COUNCILLORS' ENQUIRIES
In response to Councillor Dasko’s request for information on the boat launch fee rollout plan, Anna Cascioli, Director, Facilities and Property Management, advised that a soft launch with consistent messaging is planned for May, and enforcement commencing in June to allow for public adjustment. Councillor Dasko requested that signage be installed as soon as possible, and Ms. Cascioli confirmed that signage has been prepared and will be installed by Friday.
