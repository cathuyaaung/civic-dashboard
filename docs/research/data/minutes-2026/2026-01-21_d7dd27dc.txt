7.1
Imran Hasan, Chair, Peel Crime Stoppers with respect to Crime Stoppers Month and 50th Year Anniversary (Presentation Added)
1. Peel Crim Stoppers - January 21, 2026.pdf
Harry Pedwell, Vice Chair of Peel Crime Stoppers and Minaj Jivraj, provided background of Peel Crime Stoppers operations, and history, crime prevention and public safety presentations, community engagements and initiatives. and noted that January is Crime Stoppers month and that the program is celebrating their 50th Anniversary. Mr. Pedwell sought Committee's collaboration to advertise of Crime Stoppers on digital signs and installing signs throughout the city to encourage public participation in reporting crime.
Robert Trewartha, Director, Strategic Communications & Initiatives explained that the City has a 10% allocation of digital signs and noted that there is a cost associated with advertising. Mr. Trewartha suggested that staff could work with Crime Stoppers to provide space to promote their program for a defined period whenever space is available, and that it not impede with essential city messaging.
In response to Councillor Reid's enquiry regarding whether there is data on the impact Crime Stoppers has had in the City of Mississauga, Mr. Pedwell advised that he did not have the data available, and that he would provide it to Councillor Reid.
In response to Councillor Kovac's enquiry Mr. Pedwell explained the different functions that separate them from the Police and Safe City Mississauga.
Councillor Butt suggested that Crime Stoppers identify preferred areas to install signage and work with Transportation and Works staff on the areas they wish to install signage.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0011-2026
Moved By Councillor M. Reid
That the deputation and associated presentation by Harry Pedwell and Minaz Jivraj, with
respect to Crime Stoppers Month and 50th Year Anniversary be received.
Received

2026 Provincial Pre-budget Submission
1. 2026 Provincial Pre-budget Submission - 0036-2026.pdf

Councillor Tedjo enquired on including language that emphasizes the importance to receive funding from the province in support of the LRT's operation upon the completion of the project to ensure a sustainable project for the city.
Robert Trewartha, Director, Strategic Communications and Initiatives, noted that the submissions focused on a commitment from the province on the timelines for construction completion of the downtown "loop"; and noted that although not well received, operating funding has been raised in the past, and can be added to the submissions if it is Council wish.
In response to Councillor Dasko's enquiry regarding the Port Credit Harbour Marina "Marina", Robert Trewartha, Director, Strategic Communications and Initiatives advised that we are awaiting the completion of the Environment Assessment process in order to assign dollar amounts for submission.
Councillor Hart spoke regarding the PILT formula and requested that a submission be added to highlight that the per passenger dollar amount and cap has been unchanged and emphasize that there is no assessment on cargo volume despite the significant amount of cargo volume.
Mayor Parrish raised concerns with the outdated formula, the cargo assessment and would like to include reference to Bill 106, the Fair Municipal Finance Act, 1997 that states all municipalities will pay market value on municipal property taxes. Mayor Parrish requested that the use of federal lands to eccelerate building also be added to the submissions.
