Melissa Rydahl, Manager, Aquatics and Heather Rusiniak, Supervisor, Aquatics with respect to Aquatics: Water Safety Awareness Day
1. WSAD Aquatics Presentation.pdf
Melissa Rydahl, Manager, Aquatics, and Heather Rusiniak, Supervisor, Aquatics, provided an overview of Water Safety Awareness Day, highlighting drowning prevention, public education efforts, and the City's ongoing water safety initiatives.
Members of Committee asked the following questions:
clarification regarding drowning statistics, noting that drowning is among the top three preventable causes of death among children according to the Canadian Drowning Report.
whether the City offers a life jacket lending program and whether opportunities could be explored with community partners to expand access to life-saving equipment.
explore opportunities for additional adult swimming programs for newcomers.
opportunities to enhance water safety awareness throughout the community through the City's partnership with the Fire Department.
whether Swim to Survive videos are shown to parents of participating children and, if not, suggested that staff explore opportunities to share them with parents.
Melissa Rydahl, Manager, Aquatics, and Heather Rusiniak, Supervisor, Aquatics, responded to questions from Members of Committee and advised that staff would explore suggestions regarding expanding access to life-saving equipment, increasing adult swimming program opportunities for newcomers, and sharing the Swim to Survive video with parents.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0337-2026
Moved By Councillor A.Tedjo
That the deputation and associated presentation by Melissa Rydahl, Manager, Aquatics and Heather Rusiniak, Supervisor, Aquatics with respect to Aquatics: Water Safety Awareness Day be received.

Advancing a Low Road Salt Strategy for Winter Maintenance in the City of Mississauga
1. Advancing a Low Road Salt Strategy for Winter Maintenance in the City of Mississauga - 0336-2026.pdf
In response to Councillor Tedjo’s enquiry, Christopher Tombino, Senior Manager, Works Administration, Operations and Maintenance, advised that three of the four initiatives would proceed, with associated financial impacts to be incorporated into the 2027 budget and that the current year costs can be absorbed within the existing budget.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0350-2026
Moved By Councillor S. Dasko
That staff be directed to implement the proposed salt reduction initiatives, outlined in the report entitled “Advancing a Low Road Salt Strategy for Winter Maintenance in the City of Mississauga” dated June 2, 2026, from the Commissioner of Transportation and Works, including:
Expansion of the direct liquid brine anti-icing program;
Implementation of a “plow-first” strategy on select roadways;
A pilot project using magnesium-treated salt on sidewalks and bus stops; and
Halophyte planting trial to mitigate salt impacts in a selected location;
That, if approved, staff include the estimated operating and capital budget in the 2027 budget for Council’s consideration and approval at that time; and,
That staff report back to Council on the outcomes of the initiatives, at an appropriate time, following their implementation.
