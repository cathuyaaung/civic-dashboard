6.1
Item 8.2 - Ashmeen Soin, Resident
1. 52FrontSt.pdf
Ashmeen Soin, Resident spoke to Item 8.1 and expressed concerns, noting that their property is directly adjacent to the proposed rear drive and carport. The resident identified the following concerns:
Potential flooding risks that could result in damage to their home
Proximity of water to basement windows, as the dwelling is approximately two feet from the shared property line
That multiple elements appear to have been implemented prior to approval and without confirmed compliance
Potential future conversion of the gravel surface to concrete
A. Soin respectfully requested that the Port Credit Heritage Conservation District Subcommittee not approve any non-compliant work and require the removal of any existing non-compliant elements.
RECOMMENDATION PCHCD-0001-2026
Moved By R. Doyle
That the deputation and presentation by Ashmeen Soin, Resident regarding item 8.2 Request to Alter a Heritage Designated Property at 52 Front Street South (Ward 1) be received.
Received

8.2
Request to Alter a Heritage Designated Property at 52 Front Street South (Ward 1)
1. Request to Alter the Heritage Designated Property at 52 Front Street South.pdf
2. Appendix 1 52 Front St Drawings_Redacted.pdf
3. Appendix 2 52 Front St. South - HIA Addendum - 2026 04 13.pdf
4. Appendix 3 52 Front St S.pdf
Members engaged in discussion of the following concerns in regards to the request to alter the property and noted the following:
Carport: Members questioned the necessity and proposed location of the carport, noting potential impacts on site layout, functionality, and visual compatibility.
Landscaping: Members discussed the removal of the existing concrete pathway and its replacement with shrubs and plantings, and requested clarification on the scope and details of the landscaping plan.
Fencing and Lighting: Members raised concerns regarding the proposed fencing, including whether lighting will be incorporated, emphasizing the importance of adequate illumination and compatibility with surrounding properties.
Abushak Rajgor, MEM Engineering, advised that additional information and clarification will be provided to address the Committee’s concerns, including details related to any proposed removals, fencing specifications, lighting provisions, and the overall landscaping plan.
RECOMMENDATION PCHCD-0003-2026
Moved By R. Denhollander
That the request to alter the property at 52 Front Street South (Ward 1), as outlined in the memorandum from John Dunlop, Manager, Indigenous Relations, Culture and Heritage Planning, dated May 22, 2026, be approved, subject to the following:
That the existing wooden fencing be approved rather than the proposed and that further clarification be provided regarding the metal fencing component, including any proposed lighting details;
That the proposed landscaping be approved in accordance with the zoning requirement of a minimum of forty percent;
That the board and batten siding be approved as proposed by the owner; and
That the proposed carport component be refused.
Received
