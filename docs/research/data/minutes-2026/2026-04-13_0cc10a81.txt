DEPUTATIONS
6.1
Vyshnavi Giri with respect to Student Accessibility Project on Sidewalk Obstructions
1. ScootClear_City.pdf
Vyshnavi Giri, Resident, presented on Scootclear and spoke to the application highlights including:
report obstructions features
AI validation
hot spot mapping that highlights high risk areas and key problem locations;
safety dashboard which provides various analytics; and
future plans for Scootclear including an integration with google maps, re-routing features, and real time notifications.
In response to Committee Members spoke to the availability of the app, timeline for reports to show up on the app and fluid reporting for brief obstructions. Further, it was noted that this would be referred back to the City's Information Technology staff.
In response, V. Giri noted 10 to 15 minutes for reports to be communicated on the app and that once an obstruction is reported it is mapped.
RECOMMENDATION AAC-0001-2026
Moved By M. Reid
That the deputation and presentation by Vyshnavi Giri, resident with respect to Student Accessibility Project on Sidewalk Obstructions, be received.
Received

6.3
Suzanne Holder, Manager, Customer Experience and Innovation with respect to the MiWay Digital Screen Displays
1. MiWay Digital Screens 1.pdf
Suzanne Holder, Manager, Customer Experience and Innovation presented on the digital screens at MiWay terminals and spoke to the highlights of the screens to include:
bus arrival and departure information
locations where the digital screens are currently located and plans for expansion
screen design features and colour contrast
departure and bus capacity notifications
contact information for MiWay if assistance is needed; and
the approach to the project with included accessible features.
Subcommittee members spoke in favour of the presentation and provided feedback on the use of the colour matte black for the digital screen, enhancing the platform number on the screen, and availability at the airport on their screen.
In response, S. Holder noted the request regarding the airport screen is out of scope for the project but that staff would take back the Committee's feedback for further discussion including the third party screen.
RECOMMENDATION AAC-0002-2026
Moved By H. Karkour
That the deputation and presentation by Suzanne Holder, Manager, Customer Experience and Innovation with respect to the MiWay Digital Screen Displays, be received.
Received
6.4
Alana Tyers, Manager, Service Development MiWay with respect to the 2025 MiWay Annual Accessibility Report
1. MiWay's 2025 Accessibility Report - AAC Presentation_Accessible.pdf
Alana Tyers, Manager, Service Development presented on the MiWay 2025 Annual Accessibility report and noted its highlights including:
joining of the global Sunflower Initiative
recognition at the Annual Canadian Urban Transit Association where they received an award for the Equity, Diversity and inclusion for the Sunflower Program
the transition of the fleet to orange
enhanced accessibility through destination sign improvement phase
standardized signage for route information.
infrastructure improvements and bus loop design
stop improvements across the City
improved wheelchair securement system; and
training and accessible customer service through the Care Own and Do model.
In response to S. Viera, A. Tyers spoke to the destination signs and noted the feedback would be brought back to staff and advised members that focus would be made on specific routes to monitor services.
RECOMMENDATION AAC-0003-2026
Moved By S. Viera
That the deputation and presentation by Alana Tyers, Manager, Service Department with respect to the 2025 MiWay Annual Accessibility Report, be received.
Received
6.5
Dan Sadler, Manager, Standards, Training and Compliance with respect to the 2025 Annual Accessibility Report
1. 2025 Accessibility Annual Status Update.pdf
Dan Sadler, Manager, Standards, Training and Compliance spoke to the key highlights of the 2025 Annual Accessibility Report and six focus areas to include:
stronger accountability across the organization and accessibility being embedded into decision making processes
equitable customer service including the expansion of the Sunflower Program and launch of the Ride Kind campaign
digitally inclusive communications including enhancing website accessibility and advancing digital standards
reducing barriers in hiring practices including inclusive hiring tools
accessible movement with transit improvements and upgrades; and
the advancement of universal designs including the launch of the Facility Accessibility Design Guidelines 2.0, major accessibility upgrades and constructing a new barrier free fire station.
RECOMMENDATION AAC-0004-2026
Moved By Councillor M. Mahoney
That the deputation and presentation by Dan Sadler, Manager, Standards, Training and Compliance with respect to the 2025 Annual Accessibility Report, be received.
Received
