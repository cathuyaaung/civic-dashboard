6.1
Item 9.3 - Dan Eylon, Director, ERA Architects Inc.
1. 51-57 Dundas Street West - Conservation Strategy.pdf
Dan Eylon, Director, ERA Architects Inc., spoke to the proposed development and conservation strategy and noted that the eastern extent of the structure had been identified as heritage and that site investigations of the building confirmed the heritage fabric was intact.
In response to Councillor D. Damerla's inquiry, D. Eylon spoke to the work involved for the assessment impact and evaluation of options and design balance and planning that would anticipate the BRT's right-of-way extension.
In response to Councillor D. Damerla, John Dunlop, Manager, Indigenous Relations, Culture and Heritage Planning spoke to what was being approved at the committee meeting and noted that a conservation plan would come forward at an appropriate point in time. Further, J. Dunlop spoke to the potential right-of-way requirement and movement of the facade of the building.
In response to Terry Ward, Citizen Member, D. Eylon spoke to the heritage criteria threshold for designation in relation to the development.
RECOMMENDATION HAC-0001-2026
Moved By T. Ward
That the deputation and presentation by Dan Eylon, Director, ERA Architects with respect to item 9.3 - Request to Alter the Property at 51-55 Dundas Street West (Ward 7), Protected with a Notice of Intent to Designate, be received.
Received

Request to Alter the Property at 51-55 Dundas Street West (Ward 7), Protected with a Notice of Intent to Designate
1. Request to Alter 51 Dundas Street West (Ward 7).pdf
2. Appendix 1 Heritage Impact Assessment.pdf
D. Cook, Citizen Member spoke in favour of the proposed development and incorporation of the historical building.
Councillor D. Damerla spoke to the proposed development and noted her opposition.
In response to Councillor D. Damerla, John Dunlop, Manager, Indigenous Relations, Culture and Heritage Planning spoke to the conservation plan, timeline for development and final integration of the heritage building into the development.
RECOMMENDATION HAC-0005-2026
Moved By T. Ward
That the request to alter the property at 51-55 Dundas Street West (Ward 7), protected with a Notice of Intent to Designate, as per the report from the Commissioner of Planning and Building, dated November 27, 2025, be approved with the following conditions:
That a Conservation Plan is submitted to the satisfaction of the Manager, Indigenous Relations, Culture and Heritage Planning for the restoration and reconstruction (where necessary) of the 1913 commercial portion of the building; and
That a letter of credit is submitted based on the cost estimate of the conservation work, to be returned when the work is complete and the building ready for occupancy, to the satisfaction of the Manager, Indigenous Relations, Culture and Heritage Planning.
Approved
