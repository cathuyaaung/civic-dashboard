DEPUTATIONS
6.1
Sonya Bubas, Project Leader, Transportation, City of Mississauga with respect to Old Derry Road and Old Creditview Road Class Environmental Assessment Study
1. Old Derry Rd and Old Creditview Rd Class Environmental Assessment.pdf
Sonya Bubas, Project Leader, Transportation and David Hiett, M. Engineer, CIMA presented on the proposed rehabilitation project for Old Credit View Bridge and Levi Bridge and spoke to their respective rehabilitation noting that the Old Credit View bridge is exceeding its service life and would require replacement and that the Levi Bridge would require rehabilitation at a later date. S. Bubas further spoke to various assessments which included a Cultural Heritage Assessment, stage 1 and 2 archaeological studies and advised members of a potential 2027 completion date. S. Bubas advised subcommittee members that a public information session would be held on May 27, 2026.
Subcommittee members addressed concerns with the potential relocation of Levi Bridge, increased traffic and the design of the new Old Credit View bridge that would retain the heritage characteristics of the current bridge.
RECOMMENDATION MVHCD-0001-2026
Moved By K. Van Wart
That the deputation and presentation of Sonya Buba, Project Leader, Transportation and David Hiett, P. Eng., CIMA with respect to Old Derry Road and Old Creditview Road Class Environmental Assessment Study, be received.
Received
6.2
Item 8.1 - Megan Hobson, Hobson Built Heritage
1. HPA Presentation- 7059 Second Line West .pdf
Megan Hobson, Heritage Planner, Hobson Built Heritage presented on the proposed alterations and design for the addition to Rotherglen School to accommodate additional classrooms. M. Hobson, presented the design concept and spoke to the integration of the addition to the existing heritage home and exterior and interior design features which would have accessible access including an elevator.
Subcommittee members spoke in opposition of the alterations and noted concerns with massing, size, materials of the addition and tree preservation.
RECOMMENDATION MVHCD-0002-2026
Moved By G. Laughton
That the deputation and associated presentation by Megan Hobson, Heritage Planner, Hobson Built Heritage with respect to item 8.1 Alteration to the Heritage Designated Property at 7059 Second Line West and 929 Old Derry Road (Ward 11), be received.
Received
