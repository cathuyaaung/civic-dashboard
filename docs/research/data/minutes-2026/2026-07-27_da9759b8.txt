PUBLIC MEETING RECOMMENDATION REPORT (WARD 11)
1. Recommendation Report - OZ-OPA 24-8 W11 - 120, 128, 142, 154, 158 Queen St S and 169 Crumbie St - 0381-2026.pdf
2. Attachment 1.pdf
3. Item 6.3 - Staff Presentation - OPA OZ 24 8 W 11 Class 4.pdf
To classify the lands as Class 4 Area in accordance with the Environmental Noise Guidelines - Stationary and Transportation - Approval and Planning (NPC-300)
120, 128, 142, 154, 158 Queen Street South and 169 Crumbie Street
West side of Queen Street South, south of Britannia Road West, north of Thomas Street
Owner: Dezen Realty Company Limited
File: OZ/OPA 24-8 W11
Councillor Butt requested clarification on the purpose of the noise exemption application and the reasons it is before the Committee. Jennifer Renaud-Nicholson, Lead Planner, explained that the development application was approved by the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT) last month and that the current application relates specifically to noise mitigation requirements. J. Renaud-Nicholson noted that stationary noise is a significant concern due to the proximity of the commercial building and that the proposed mitigation measures are not desirable. As a result, a Class 4 Area designation is being requested.
RECOMMENDATION PDC-0041-2026
Moved By Councillor B. Butt
Tha t Council classify the lands at 120, 128, 142, 154, 158 Queen Street South and 169 Crumbie Street as a Class 4 Area in accordance with the Environmental Noise Guidelines - Stationary and Transportation Sources - Approval and Planning (NPC - 300).
YES (11)
Councillor A. Tedjo, Councillor S. Dasko, Councillor C. Fonseca, Councillor J. Kovac, Councillor N. Hart, Councillor J. Horneck, Councillor D. Damerla, Councillor M. Mahoney, Councillor M. Reid, Councillor S. McFadden, and Councillor B. Butt
Carried (11 to 0)
