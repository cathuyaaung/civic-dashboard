DEPUTATIONS
Any member of the public interested in making a deputation to an item listed on the agenda must register by calling 905-615-3200 ext. 5418 or by emailing: [email protected] by Tuesday January 27 by 4:00 pm
Each Deputation to Mississauga School Traffic Safety Action Committee is limited to speaking not more than 10 minutes.
Pursuant to Section 57.1 of the Council Procedure By-law 0044-2022, as amended:
Deputations shall be received and the matter shall be referred to staff for a report, unless there is a resolution or recommendation passed to “receive” the Deputation. After a Deputation is completed, Members shall each have one opportunity to make a preamble statement and ask questions to the Deputant(s) or staff for clarification purposes only, and without debate.
6.1
Nicole Oliveira, Resident
Item 9.6
Nicole Oliveira stated that she does not agree with the committee’s recommendation of not to have a crossing guard placed at location.
Councillor Butt discussed that there were no concerns, noting that afternoon observations occur outside peak rush hour, traffic is lighter, and Irene Chu Committee member was onsite inspector and observed the area to be quiet during the afternoon. Councillor Butt suggested conducting another afternoon inspection at a later date. N. Olveira disagreed with the committee’s members discussion and requested to be notified once a date and time for the next inspection is scheduled.
N. Olveira also requested to submit video footage of the observation to staff; S. Duffin agreed to receive the video through the Monica Ruffolo, Legislative Coordinator
RECOMMENDATION MSTSAC-0001-2026
Moved By Councillor B. Butt
That the deputation by Nicole Oliveira, Resident with respect to the results of the site inspection conducted on December 18, 2025 at the intersection of River Grove Avenue and Willow Way for the students of Hazel McCallion Senior Public School, be received.

9.8
Site Inspection Report-Elm Drive Public School (Ward 7)
1. Elm Drive Public School.pdf
Site inspection report for the site inspection conducted on January 22, 2026 at the intersection of Kariya Drive at the Elm Drive Public School Driveway for the students attending Elm Drive Public School
Amira Alvi wanted to ensure that recommendation of 4.a be noted to should there be any future concerns.
RECOMMENDATION MSTSAC-0009-2026
Moved By A. Alvi
1. That the warrants have not been met for the placement of a school crossing guard at the condominium at 3525 Kariya Drive north of the Elm Drive Public School driveway.
2. Transportation and Works be requested to:
a. Implement a winter maintained pedestrian path on the east side of Kariya Drive south from 3525 Kariya Drive to align with the northeast corner of Elm Drive Public School opposite on the west side of Kariya Drive.
b. Add “No Stopping" Monday to Friday September to June, 8:00–9:30 am and 3:00–4:30 pm prohibitions on the east side of Kariya Drive to allow for better traffic flow.
c. Provide a timeline for the extension of Kariya Drive south to Central Parkway.
d.Give consideration to the implementation of a pedestrian crossover on Kariya Drive south of the school driveway to align with the crosswalk on school property at the northeast corner of Elm Drive Public School.
3. That the Peel District School Board be requested to paint a crosswalk on Elm Drive Public School school property to align the sidewalk extension on the east side of Kariya Drive with the northeast corner of Elm Drive Public School.
4. That the principal of Elm Drive Public School be requested to:
a. Remind parents and students to remain on the east side of Kariya Drive and cross to the school on the west side of Kariya Drive south of the school driveway where there is no vehicle traffic once the winter maintained pedestrian path is extended or cross Kariya Drive at the signalized intersection with the crossing guards at Kariya Drive and Elm Drive.
b.Consider allowing parents to exit school property using the driveway leading to Central Parkway to avoid the congestion on Kariya Drive.
5. That Mississauga School Traffic Safety Action Committee be requested to re-inspect once recommendation 2. and 3. have been implemented.
