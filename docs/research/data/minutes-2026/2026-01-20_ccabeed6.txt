CORRESPONDENCE
11.1
An email dated January 19, 2026 from Mike Harris, on behalf of Applewood Hills & Heights Residents Association (AHHRA) with questions and comments regarding the City’s 2026 Proposed Budget
Councillor Fonseca read the correspondence from the Applewood Hills & Heights Residents' Association (AHHRA) and requested Finance staff to provide high level answers to the questions, noted below:
1. Why do we have $30M in Investment & $60M in Debt?
Marisa Chiu, Commissioner, Corporate Services, Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer spoke to the debt balance, outstanding debt projections, repayment, interest and debt; as well as the cash investment portfolio and rationale regarding use, and spoke to the policy on debt.
2. Why did we incur an 8% increase in Labour, Union & non-union wages?
M. Chiu spoke to the labour adjustment and combination of increases including breaking down the components related to the adjustment. Geoff Wright, City Manager and CAO noted that staff negotiate on behalf of the City and spoke to negotiations, arbitration process, and practices.
3. Could we save money by purchasing mid-sized buses & use them on less busy routes or at less busy times?
G. Wright spoke to the fleet size, composition of fleet, operational decisions by MiWay, scheduling software for route assignments, and that staff are looking to introduce on-demand service in the future; and further noted that majority of costs are associated with labour vs. vehicle size.
4. The City of Vancouver & the Town of Orillia have recently announced a 0% Property Tax increase for 2026. Why can't Mississauga do the same? The City of Vancouver also implemented an Auditor General (AG) a few years ago. The ROI they have achieved with the AG Office is posted as: 2023 a 160% ROI and in 2024 a 360% ROI. We request that the City of Mississauga also look to hire an Auditor General (AG) for the city for 2026 or 2027 at the latest ‘to provide independent, objective oversight of city operations, ensuring accountability, transparency, and value-for-money in the stewardship of public funds.’
Councillor Fonseca noted the Applewood Hills & Heights Residents' Association raised the matter at the Region of Peel regarding hiring an Auditor General at the City and spoke to how a value for money audit could be carried.
M. Chiu noted that staff can report on the pros and cons of an auditor general and outlined factors such as the scope of the Region’s auditor general, benefits to benchmarking, cost associated with auditor general and budget impacts.
Mayor Parrish inquired about the scope of the Audit Committee, hiring an external company to audit the City and costs associated with it. In response, G. Wright spoke to the Audit Committee mandate, reporting structure, compliance audits scope, and outlined options for auditing.
Councillor Horneck spoke to the Audit Committee and suggested seeing how the Auditor General at the Region is working before considering at the City level.
Councillor Fonseca noted that she will work with Finance staff regarding providing written responses to the Applewood Hills & Heights Residents' Association and direction was provided to staff to share written responses with Members of Budget Committee.
RECOMMENDATION BC-0032-2026
Moved By Councillor C. Fonseca
That the email dated January 19, 2026 from Mike Harris, on behalf of Applewood Hills & Heights Residents Association (AHHRA) with questions and comments regarding the City’s 2026 Proposed Budget, be received.
Received
