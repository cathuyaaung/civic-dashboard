DEPUTATION
5.1
Wing-See Wu, Project Manager with respect to a Review of South Common Community Centre and Library Playground
1. Redevelopment of SCCL_ Playground.pdf
Wing-See Wu, Project Manager spoke to the redevelopment of South Common Community Centre and playgrounds and noted the following:
playground structures and location
accessible routes to the building and park locations
access to the splash pad
materials to be used at the primary playground as well as concrete seat wall, interactive sensory play features, play structure features.
Subcommittee members requested the following:
signage in two locations
low level plants or set backs along walkways
colour contrasting
use of braille on tactile play areas
orientation map with symbols or icons, QR codes and information links.
In response, Wing-See Wu noted:
that walkways would have cane detectable edges
staff would look at the request for varied seating heights in the concrete seating area
that there were no bushes or plants in the primary play area only in the secondary play area but staff would take into consideration the request for low level plants.
RECOMMENDATION FADS-0001-2026
Moved By C. Chafe
That the deputation and presentation by Wing-See Wu, Project Manager with respect to the review of South Common Community Centre and Library Playground, be received.
Received
5.2
Greg Baskin, Senior Associate and Senior Project Leader and Julia Steele, Project Manager, Landscape Planning Limited with respect to design of the future park at the former Grand Highland Golf Course
1. Grand Highland Park FADS1.pdf
Greg Baskin, Senior Associate, Landscape Planning Limited spoke to the background of the project, location, topography and history of the site and noted as follows:
vehicular access
container washroom location
lighting and materials to be used on walkways
bench and gathering locations
site furnishings
surface materials for trails, plazas, spray pad and activity hub
two accessible parking spaces one Type A and one Type B
activity hub features and play area surface materials
look out point
community garden design and accessible picnic tables
project timeline.
Subcommittee members provided feedback and noted the following:
request for increased accessible parking spaces
drop off and pick up area
location of garbage cans
signage locations
accessible garden planters
varied height of stone seating
wayfinding and signage
bike and scooter parking areas
wheelchair charging stations
shelter areas.
In response, G. Baskin spoke to the design of accessible planters, location of e-scooter and e-bike parking and the incorporation of a drop off area.
RECOMMENDATION FADS-0002-2026
Moved By C. Lowe
That the deputation and presentation by Greg Baskin, Senior Associate and Senior Project Leader, Landscape Planning Limited with respect to the design of the future park at the former Grand Highland Golf Course, be received.
Received
