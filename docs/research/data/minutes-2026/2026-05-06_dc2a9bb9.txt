7.5
Marek Ruta, Chairman, Carassauga Festival of Cultures with respect to an update on the Annual Festival returning on May 23 and 24, 2026
Marek Ruta, Chairman, Carassauga Festival of Cultures, provided an overview of the 2026 festival, highlighted the theme “Sport and Cultural Unity,” and expressed appreciation to sponsors and City partners.
In response to Councillor Fonseca’s enquiry, M. Ruta advised that discounted advance tickets include free MiWay transit, can be exchanged for a festival passport, and there is no additional cost for the bike tour. He added that a robust marketing campaign will promote the festival.
In response to Mayor Parrish’s enquiry, M. Ruta confirmed the Ukrainian pavilion will be held at a Ukrainian church and feature a twin cities display.
In response to Councillor Hart's enquiry regarding what types of sports are being showcased, M. Ruta outlined that each participating country will feature sport-specific activities reflective of their cultural heritage.
In response to Councillor Kovac's enquiry regarding the absence of Italy and what distinguishes this year’s festival, M. Ruta explained that Italian organizers were unable to participate and noted that the festival continues to grow post-pandemic, with organizers securing larger spaces.
In response to Councillor Dasko's enquiry regarding how Members of Council could assist in promoting the festival and whether a communications toolkit is available, M. Ruta advised that promotional materials can be shared to help spread awareness.
Councillor Damerla left at 9:34 AM
RECOMMENDATION GC-0253-2026
Moved By Councillor J. Kovac

Festival of Cultures with respect to an update on the Annual Festival returning on May 23 and 24, 2026 be received.
7.6
Item 10.2 Nadia Paladino, Director, Parks, Forestry and Environment
Nadia Paladino, Director, Parks, Forestry and Environment, provided an overview of the Equip Sport program, including its spring 2026 launch, operations, equipment lockers, app access, and site selection. She noted that public communications will be shared once locations are finalized.
In response to Councillor Fonseca enquiry, N. Paladino advised that skipping ropes will be included, while rackets will not, and confirmed that location and equipment details will be communicated.
In response to Councillor Reid enquiry, N. Paladino advised that funding is provided through Equip Sport and that equipment may be relocated if theft occurs.
Quentin Sandrini, EquipSport, confirmed that data is securely stored, anonymized, and compliant with regulations.
In response to Councillor Horneck enquiry, N. Paladino advised that sites are selected based on criteria, with one location per ward and potential for future expansion.
In response to Councillor Hart's enquiry, N. Paladino advised that cricket bats are not included in the pilot and confirmed the program is app-based, free to use, and does not require payment information.
Item 10.2 was brought forward and voted on.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0254-2026
Moved By Councillor C. Fonseca
