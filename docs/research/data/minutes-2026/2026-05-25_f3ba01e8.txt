DEPUTATION
6.1
Darlene Utarid, Manager, Capital Design & Construction with respect to Single Use Washrooms at Mississauga City Hall
1. Single Use Washrooms Overview.pdf
Darlene Utarid, Manager, Capital Design & Construction spoke to the renovations of City washroom facilities to single use washrooms and noted the following:
challenges based on space
locations for the renovated bathrooms on the ground floor, 4th, 8th and 12th floor
the design elements and accessible features
that the washrooms would be gender neutral
align with FADS 2.0 standards
use of existing footprints
universal washroom on the 8th floor
and continued gender specific washrooms would be available; and
anticipated completion dates for 2026.
Committee members inquired about the following concerns:
availability of feminine products in single use washrooms
wave soap dispensers rather than push dispensers; and
use of unscented soaps.
RECOMMENDATION FADS-0005-2026
Moved By A. Zaidi
That the deputation and presentation by Darlene Utarid, Manager, Capital Design & Construction with respect to Single Use Washrooms at Mississauga City Hall, be received.
