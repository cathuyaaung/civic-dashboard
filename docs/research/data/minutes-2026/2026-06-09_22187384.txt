9.2
Request to Alter a Heritage Designated Property at 7059 Second Line West (Ward 11)
1. Request to Alter a Heritage Designated Property at 7059 Second Line West (Ward 11).pdf
2. Appendix 1 HIA_7059 Second Line West, Meadowvale, Mississauga_26 Mar 2026 (1).pdf
Jim Holmes, Citizen Member spoke to the proposed amendments to the recommendation.
Councillor Brad Butt, Chair noted that the correspondence submitted by Megan Hobson, Hobson Built Heritage did refer to some of the proposed recommendations.
RECOMMENDATION HAC-0049-2026
Moved By J. Holmes
That the request to alter the Part V heritage designated property at 7059 Second Line West (Ward 11), as per the Report from the Commissioner of Planning & Building, dated April 22, 2026, be approved with the following conditions:
That the final designs adhere to the Meadowvale Village Heritage Conservation District Plan guidelines or justification be provided as to why they cannot be met;
That the final architectural and landscaping drawings be presented to the Heritage Advisory Committee by staff for review prior to final review and approval to ensure consistency with the District Plan and Heritage Impact Assessment; and
That a Tree Protection Plan be provided and implemented for the protection of existing trees within the construction zone.
Approved
