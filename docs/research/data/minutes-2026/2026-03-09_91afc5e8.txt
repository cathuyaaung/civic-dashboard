PUBLIC MEETING RECOMMENDATION REPORT (WARD 11)
1. Recommendation Report - OZ-OPA 25-3 W11 - 64 and 66 Thomas Street - 0049-2026.pdf
2. Appendix 1 - Public Meeting Information Report May 26 2025.pdf
3. Item 6.1 Staff Presentation OZ OPA 25-3 W11.pdf
Official Plan Amendment and Rezoning applications to permit three apartment buildings with heights of 12, 18 and 22 storeys, containing 1,043 dwelling units
64 & 66 Thomas Street, 95 Joymar Drive & 65 Tannery Street, northeast corner of Thomas Street and Joymar Drive
Owner: Dezen Realty Company Limited
File: OZ/OPA 25-3 W11
Jennifer Renaud‑Nicholson, Planner, provided an overview of the report, subject lands, site context, proposed development, evaluation of the applications, and next steps.
Karthikeyan Palanivelappan, Resident, raised concerns regarding the adequacy of infrastructure to support the proposed development.
Brian Chapman, Resident,raised concerns regarding traffic and street parking on Joymar Drive and within the surrounding area. B. Chapman acknowledged the need for quality housing but expressed concerns related to building height and density, capacity for fire and police services, lack of parks and green space, and the insufficient number of proposed two‑ and three‑bedroom units.
RECOMMENDATION PDC-0013-2026
Moved By Councillor B. Butt
1. The City Council direct Legal Services, representative s from the appropriate City Departments and any necessary consultants to oppose the subject applications under File OZ/OPA 25-3 W11 to permit three apartment buildings with heights of 12, 18 and 22 storey s contain ing 1,043 dwelling units before the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT) consistent with the recommendations outlined in the report dated February 18 , 2026 from the Commissioner of Planning and Building, that concludes that the proposed official plan and rezoning are not acceptable from a planning perspective and should not be approved.
2. That City Council provide the Planning and Building Department with the authority to instruct Legal Services on modifications to the position deemed necessary during or before the Ontario Land Tribunal hearing process, however, if there is a potential for settlement then a report shall be brought back to Council by Legal Services.
3. That 2 (two) oral submissions be received.
YES (11)
Councillor S. Dasko, Councillor C. Fonseca, Councillor J. Kovac, Mayor Parrish, Councillor D. Damerla, Councillor M. Mahoney, Councillor S. McFadden, Councillor J. Horneck, Councillor M. Reid, Councillor B. Butt, and Councillor N. Hart
Carried (11 to 0)

RECOMMENDATION REPORT (WARD 7)
1. OZ OPA 24-14 W7 - 2463-2469 Mimosa Row - 0126-2026.pdf
2. Appendix 1.pdf
3. Item 6.2 Staff Presentation OZ OPA 24-14 W7.pdf
Official Plan Amendment and Rezoning applications to permit six freehold townhouses
2463 and 2469 Mimosa Row
Northeast corner of Floradale Drive and Mimosa Row
Owner: Beata Lis, Margaret Lis and Zygmunt Lis
File: OZ/OPA 24-14 W7
Michal Stolarczyk, Planner, provided an overview of the subject property, concept plan, applicable Mississauga Official Plan policies, and staff recommendations.
Morgan Dundas, Associate, Sajecki Planning, thanked Planning staff and the Ward 7 Councillor for their support throughout the process and highlighted the need for family‑oriented housing.
Councillor Damerla thanked Planning staff and emphasized the importance of supporting townhouse development.
RECOMMENDATION PDC-0014-2026
Moved By Councillor D. Damerla
1. That application s under F ile OZ/OPA 24-14 W7, Beata Lis, Margaret Lis and Zygmunt Lis, 2463 and 2469 Mimosa Row to amend Mississauga Official Plan to Residential Low Density II ; and to change the zoning to R M 5-Exception ( S treet T ownhouses – E xception) to permit six freehold townhouses , be approved i n accordance with the provisions contained in the staff report dated February 1 8 , 2026 from the Commissioner of Planning and Building, and that staff b e directed to return to City Council with the appropriate implementing documents and by-laws for approval .
2. That the applicant agree to satisfy all the requirements of the City , including entering into a Development Agreement, and any other external agency concerned with the development.
3.That Planning and Development Committee’s approval of the rezoning application under File OZ/OPA 24-14 W7 be considered null and void, and a new development application be required unless a zoning by-law is passed within 18 months of the Council decision.
4.That notwithstanding that subsequent to the public meeting, changes to the application have been proposed, Council considers that the changes do not require further notice and, therefore, pursuant to the provisions of subsection 34(17) of the Planning Act , any further notice regarding the proposed amendment is hereby waived.
5. That 1 (one) oral submission be received.
YES (11)
Councillor S. Dasko, Councillor C. Fonseca, Councillor J. Kovac, Mayor Parrish, Councillor D. Damerla, Councillor M. Mahoney, Councillor S. McFadden, Councillor J. Horneck, Councillor M. Reid, Councillor B. Butt, and Councillor N. Hart
Carried (11 to 0)

PUBLIC MEETING RECOMMENDATION REPORT (WARD 1)
1. Biocca Homes Inc - 0121-2026.pdf
2. Appendix 1 - Supplementary Information.pdf
3. Item 6.3 Staff Presentation - OZ 25-20 W1.pdf
Rezoning application to permit two semi-detached dwelling units
893 Seventh Street, north of Atwater Avenue, east of Cawthra Road
Owner: Biocca Homes Inc.
File: OZ 25-20 W1
David Breveglieri, Lead Planner, provided an overview of the proposal, planning context, rezoning request and evaluation process.
Jim Levac, Glenn Schnarr and Associates, thanked Planning staff, noted that the application represents gentle intensification and spoke to the rezoning of an individual lot, including frontage considerations and the potential need for further observation.
Matthew Morelli, Resident, raised concerns regarding planning impacts, reduced zoning standards, neighbourhood character, and the creation of a precedent for similar rezonings on interior residential streets.
Councillor Butt sought clarification on whether a duplex could be considered instead of two semi‑detached dwellings. Planning staff provided confirmation.
Mark Gornik, Resident, raised concerns regarding parking, cumulative impacts on the street, noise, and expressed opposition to the development. Councillor Dasko thanked M. Gornik and inquired about impacts on enjoyment of the home and stormwater runoff issues, which were confirmed by the resident.
Councillor Dasko noted concerns related to lot coverage, flooding, height, neighbourhood character, shading and parking, and advised that he would not be supporting the recommendation.
RECOMMENDATION PDC-0015-2026
Moved By Councillor B. Butt
1.That application under F ile OZ 25-20 W1 , Biocca Homes Inc. , 893 Seventh Street change the zoning to RS-Exceptio n to permit two semi-detached dwelling units , be approved i n accordance with the provisions contained in the staff report dated February 18, 2026 from the Commissioner of Planning and Building, and that staff b e directed to return to City Council with the appropriate implementing documents and by-laws for approval .
2. That the applicant agree to satisfy all the requirements of the City and any other external agency concerned with the development.
3. That Planning and Development Committee’s approval of the rezoning application under File OZ 25-20 W1 be considered null and void, and a new development application be required unless a zoning by-law is passed within 18 months of the Council decision .
4. That 3 (three) oral submissions be received.
YES (6)
Councillor C. Fonseca, Councillor J. Kovac, Mayor Parrish, Councillor M. Mahoney, Councillor B. Butt, and Councillor N. Hart
NO (4)
Councillor S. Dasko, Councillor S. McFadden, Councillor J. Horneck, and Councillor M. Reid
Carried (6 to 4)
