7.1
Samantha Fung, Founder, Music For Every Child Progress Update
1. Music For Every Child 2025 2026 Updates.pdf
Samantha Fung, Founder, Riddhima Naithani, Director of Communications and Media, and Noah Munoz Saravia, Communications Team Member provided an overview of the Music For Every Child Program, highlighting its growth across Ontario, its operational model, international presence, and global impact. She noted Mississauga’s recognition on a global stage through awards, ongoing community engagement, and requested support with media coverage and advocacy.
Councillor Dasko congratulated the group on their growth and inquired how Council could best assist with media and advocacy efforts. S. Fung encouraged attendance at community events and promotion through Councillors’ social media platforms and agreed to share event information.
Councillor Hart requested clarification on event participation, including timing and notice requirements. S. Fung advised that volunteer availability is greater during summer months and provided general context on event planning timelines.
In response to Councillor Kovac's enquiry regarding the organization’s success, S. Fung advised that strong demand for the program has driven growth, though funding remains a challenge, with ongoing efforts to engage corporate and government partners to expand programming.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0319-2026
Moved By Councillor S. Dasko
That the deputation and associated presentation by Samantha Fung, Founder, Riddhima Naithani, Director of Communications and Media, and Noah Munoz Saravia, Communications Team Member, with respect to an update on Music For Every Child Progress be received.
Received
7.2
Vito Dom Macchia, Resident with respect to Permitted On-Street Parking
1. Letter from Vito Dom Macchia - Reassess Parking Permit_Redacted.pdf
Vito Dom Macchia, resident, raised concerns regarding the misuse of permitted on-street parking. He referenced the number of parking permits allowed in other jurisdictions and requested that Committee review its current practices to ensure the permit system is not being misused.
Councillor Tedjo emphasized the need to address immediate concerns and requested a meeting with staff to discuss enforcement, noting that staff are currently undertaking a parking study with future proposed changes.
Sam Rogers, Commissioner, Transportation and Works advised the issues are known and highlighted recent updates (5-hour limit, 2–6 a.m. prohibition), noting ongoing review of other jurisdictions and a potential permanent program.
Georgios Fthenos, Director, Enforcement advised that he will follow up with the resident and advised that options, including licence-based permits, are under review.
Councillor Dasko requested that the recent changes be revisited, citing continued misuse of the permit system.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0320-2026
Moved By Councillor A.Tedjo
That the deputation and associated letter dated June 1, 2026 from Vito Dom Macchia, Resident with respect to Permitted On-Street Parking be received.
Received
7.3
Item 10.2, Dan Sadler, Manager, Accessibility Policy & Compliance
1. 2025 Accessibility Annual Status Update.pdf
Dan Sadler, Manager, Accessibility Policy & Compliance, provided an overview of the importance of accessibility, outlining current progress across the six focus areas, key achievements to date, partnerships, customer service initiatives, and future efforts to build a more inclusive Mississauga.
In response to Councillor Reid's enquiry regarding how the City is communicating these efforts and meeting or exceeding AODA requirements and which initiative he is most proud of, D. Sadler advised that outreach is conducted through social media, community events, and initiatives such as National Accessibility Week to promote awareness, and, highlighted the initiative that launched the Accessibility Plan 2.0, noting the extensive effort to incorporate best practices across the organization, as well as the expansion of the Sunflower Program, which has increased participation and awareness in the community.
In response to Councillor Fonseca's enquiry regarding volunteer and training opportunities for youth, D. Sadler provided information on how youth can access these opportunities, including high-level program pathways available through City initiatives.
In response to Councillor Kovac's enquiry on meeting accessibility targets, D. Sadler expressed confidence in achieving the required 15% target.
RECOMMENDATION GC-0321-2026
Moved By Councillor C. Fonseca
That the deputation and associated presentation by Dan Sandler, Accessibility Policy & Compliance, with respect to the Multi-Year Accessibility Plan - 2025 Accessibility Annual status update be received.
Received

COUNCILLORS' ENQUIRIES
Councillor Hart raised concerns regarding illegal dumping at parkland areas and Westwood Mall and inquired about the installation of signage to discourage illegal dumping and advise of applicable fines. Councillor Hart also noted that Westwood Mall has committed to installing additional lighting and signage as a deterrent.
Raj Sheth, Commissioner, Community Services, confirmed that signage will be installed and that staff will follow up with Councillor Hart regarding specific locations.
Councillor Tedjo requested that deputants listed on the agenda include identification of the ward they are from or the ward to which the issue pertains.
Councillor Fonseca spoke regarding parking exceptions for specific holidays and noted that, during the recent Eid celebration, residents received parking tickets.
Raj Sheth, Commissioner, Community Services, advised that he would meet with Councillor Fonseca offline to review the matter.
Councillor Damerla expressed concern about a perceived inconsistency, given that some of these plants have been removed from the provincial noxious weeds list, and noted the importance of aligning priorities, and in response to her enquiry, Nadia Paladino, Director, Parks & Forestry & Environment confirmed that Forestry’s priorities are that key invasive species, including garlic mustard, continue to be actively managed as part of ongoing efforts.
Councillor McFadden commented on parking exceptions during holidays, noting that while exceptions are permitted, the city does not allow vehicles to be parked in prohibited or illegal locations.
