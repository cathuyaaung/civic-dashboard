7.2
Lyndsay Cartwright, Research Scientist, Toronto and Region Conservation Authority and Amanda Chiasson, Research Student, Queen's University regarding Innovative Solutions to Mitigate Winter Salt Impacts on the Environment
A. Chiasson spoke to road salt as an environmental issue affecting freshwater systems through direct and indirect pathways and the use of halophyte remediation to intercept and remove salt from the soil. A. Chiasson further spoke to the phytoremediation field site results near Toronto Pearson Airport.
L. Cartwright spoke to regional collaborations, including Lake Wilcox, and stormwater desalting, identifying chloride sources, and spoke to Toronto and Region Conservation Authority’s (TRCA) board resolution regarding limited liability protection legislation for salt contractors.
Daniel Filippi, Sustainable Technologies Evaluation Program, TRCA spoke to chloride source identification, salt management plans, salt alternatives, and spoke to TRCA’s new procurement guidance for parking lot snow and ice management, review of snow and ice control practices on lots and walkways, and spoke to new pilot projects on brine usage and Smart About Salt training certification.
Committee Members commented and inquired about the following:
Measurement and collaborating with other watersheds on salt usage
Location of planting prairie grass and switch grass
Handling of harvested plant material
Plant resilience to invasive species (phragmites, narrow leaf cattail)
In response, D. Filippi and Barbara Zeeb, Professor, Royal Military College of Canada provided information on research in other jurisdictions, funding for reduced salt application rates, planting in line with runoff, native species planting and location, and composting plant material.
Councillor Mahoney, Chair spoke to opportunities for the City to participate in projects and pilots for innovative solutions to salt remediation; and suggested the presentation be shared with Transportation and Works staff.
Dianne Zimmerman, Manager, Environment noted that staff from Transportation and Works are in attendance and available to work on further investigating opportunities.
RECOMMENDATION EAC-0008-2026
Moved By Councillor Dasko
That the deputation and associated presentation from Lyndsay Cartwright, Research Scientist, Toronto and Region Conservation Authority and Amanda Chiasson, Research Student, Queen's University regarding Innovative Solutions to Mitigate Winter Salt Impacts on the Environment, be received.
Received
