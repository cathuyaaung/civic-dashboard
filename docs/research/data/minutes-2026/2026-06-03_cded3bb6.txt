NOTICE OF MOTION
15.1
A Notice of Motion to extend the Development Charges Incentives from December 31, 2027 to March 30, 2029 to include eligible residential developments (Mayor Parrish)
1. MotionDC Incentives ExtensionJune 3 Special Council Meeting (3).pdf
Mayor Parrish and Councillor Dasko spoke in support of the Notice of Motion, noting that it had previously been adopted by Regional Council.
Councillors Damerla and Horneck enquired about the anticipated costs to the City of Mississauga and the level of funding received from the federal government. Marisa Chiu, Commissioner of Corporate Services, responded and advised that there are challenges in establishing a precise cost estimate.
RESOLUTION 0116-2026
Moved By Mayor C. Parrish
Seconded By Councillor S. Dasko
WHEREAS on January 29, 2025 Council passed Resolution 0015-2025, which reduced the amount of City residential development charges (“City Residential DCs”) payable by 100% for all three(3)-bedroom units within purpose-built rental residential apartment developments and reduced City Residential DCs by 50% for all other residential developments, with building permits issued prior to November 13, 2026;
AND WHEREAS on February 11, 2026, Council passed Resolution 0031-2026, which reduced the amount of City Residential DCs payable by 100% for units with one-bedroom plus den and two-bedrooms or larger that are part of a purpose-built rental residential apartment developments with building permits issued prior to November 13, 2026, and construction has commenced within six months of permit issuance allowing for footings and foundation;
AND WHEREAS on March 11, 2026, Council passed Resolution 0054-2026, which extended the above City Residential DC incentives to include eligible developments with building permits allowing footings and foundations issued prior to December 31, 2027;
AND WHEREAS on June 1, 2026, the Province of Ontario announced the Development Charges Reduction Program (“DCRP”), a joint Federal-Provincial program designed to help municipalities reduce DCs and build infrastructure that supports new housing;
AND WHEREAS to meet DCRP criteria, a municipality will need to reduce DCs by 30% to 50% for a minimum of three (3) years for all residential building types, effective March 30, 2026;
AND WHEREAS City Council is one of the earliest adopters of residential DC reductions and the amount of reductions has exceeded DCRP program requirement;
AND WHEREAS the City’s Affordable Rental Community Improvement Plan will get over 3,600 new rental units started, including 527 affordable units;
AND WHEREAS the City has completed the pre-zoning of Major Transit Station Areas, expected to unlock up to 114,000 dwelling units, supporting approximately 227,000 residents and 47,000 jobs;
AND WHEREAS the City’s residential development pipeline indicates that over 20,000 new units could be enabled through the DCRP, including approximately 8,000 residential units that are near-term and could proceed upon permit issuance by the end of 2027, with an additional 12,000 units that could advance over the extended incentive period and beyond;
AND WHEREAS City Council desires to further extend the City Residential DC Incentives to meet the DCRP requirement regarding program duration.
Therefore be it resolved that:
1. Resolution 0015-2025 is further amended to extend the Development Charges Incentives described therein from December 31, 2027 to March 30, 2029, inclusive, to include eligible residential developments with building permits allowing footings and foundations issued pursuant to the Building Code Act, 1992 prior to March 30, 2029.
2. Resolution 0031-2026 is further amended to extend the Development Charges Incentives described therein from December 31, 2027 to March 30, 2029, inclusive, to include eligible purpose-built rental residential development with building permits allowing for footings and foundations issued pursuant to the Building Code Act, 1992 prior to March 30, 2029.
3. Sections 1 to 2 of this Motion are effective immediately following the execution of Transfer Payment Agreement(s) under the Development Charges Reduction Program (DCRP).
4. All other terms of Resolutions 0015-2025, 0031-2026, and 0054-2026 continue to apply, without amendment.
