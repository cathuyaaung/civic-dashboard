INDIGENOUS LAND STATEMENT
“We acknowledge the lands which constitute the present-day City of Mississauga as being part of the Treaty and Traditional Territory of the Mississaugas of the Credit First Nation, The Haudenosaunee Confederacy the Huron-Wendat and Wyandotte Nations. We recognize these peoples and their ancestors as peoples who inhabited these lands since time immemorial. The City of Mississauga is home to many global Indigenous Peoples.
As a municipality, the City of Mississauga is actively working towards reconciliation by confronting our past and our present, providing space for Indigenous peoples within their territory, to recognize and uphold their Treaty Rights and to support Indigenous Peoples. We formally recognize the Anishinaabe origins of our name and continue to make Mississauga a safe space for all Indigenous peoples.”
Councillor Damerla, Chair recited the Indigenous Land Statement.

OTHER BUSINESS
Councillor Martin Reid advised the Committee a letter of of support issued by the Black Opportunity Fund advocating for amendments to Bill C-9 to address Anti-Black hate.
The Committee engaged in a discussion regarding how this could be brought and
addressed at the Municipal Level:
• The committee may bring forward a letter from the Mayor proposing amendment to Bill C9.
• Any correspondence or motion should address the concerns raised regarding Bill C9.
• Consideration was given to the option of advancing Bill C9 itself as a formal motion of the committee.
• meaningful engagement with community members, ensuring participation from the broader community.
• Discussion included timelines and whether there is sufficient appetite within the committee to undertake public engagement prior to proceeding.
Abdul Qayyum Mufti, Citizen Member spoke to introducing Faith Based Equity Advisory Committee to address concerns of Inter Faith Groups and their ongoing concerns.
Rob Trewartha, Director Strategic Communications & Initiatives, advised and thanked Committee for its contributions in the production of the One Mississauga Campaign
RECOMMENDATION CRDHAC-0003-2026
