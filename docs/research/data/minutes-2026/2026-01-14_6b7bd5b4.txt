15.1
A Notice of Motion to waive the temporary zoning application costs in the amount of $5,883.00 associated with the brief 6-month extension of the current temporary use by-law for 46 William Street (Councillor Butt) (CONSENT)
1. Notice of Motion 46 William St Jan 14'26 Final.pdf
RESOLUTION 0005-2026
Moved By Councillor S. Dasko
Seconded By Councillor B. Butt
WHEREAS since 1998, a minor variance, an official plan amendment, a rezoning and temporary use applications have been approved by PDC and Council for the City-owned lands at 46 William Street, allowing for the outdoor storage of refrigeration trailers by Quickchill Mobile Refrigeration Ltd.;
AND WHEREAS the most recent application under File T-OZ 22-015 W11, City of Mississauga, 46 William Street to extend a temporary use by-law to permit the outdoor storage of refrigeration trailers for a period of three years was approved by PDC and Council in January of 2023 and will expire on January 31, 2026;
AND WHEREAS Quickchill Mobile Refrigeration Ltd. has requested another six-month extension to facilitate the relocation and moving of the aforementioned refrigeration trailers from 46 William Street, and City staff are agreeable to this brief extension of the current temporary use by-law, expiring on July 31, 2026, to accommodate the applicant’s request;
AND WHEREAS the proposed extension of the current temporary use by-law for 46 William Street will require another temporary zoning application costing $5,883.00;
AND WHEREAS Quickchill Mobile Refrigeration Ltd. has requested relief from the aforementioned application cost, and there is currently no delegated authority allowing City staff to provide relief from such application fees based on brevity of a temporary use by-law;
AND WHEREAS Quickchill Mobile Refrigeration Ltd. has been a longtime tenant without issue at 46 William Street, providing employment opportunities and making efficient use of these vacant and underutilized lands for over two decades;
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT City staff be directed to waive the temporary zoning application costs in the amount of $5,883.00 associated with the brief six-month extension of the current temporary use by-law for 46 William Street.
YES (10)
Councillor S. Dasko, Councillor C. Fonseca, Deputy Mayor and Councillor J. Kovac , Deputy Mayor and Councillor M. Mahoney, Councillor S. McFadden, Councillor A. Tedjo, Councillor J. Horneck, Councillor M. Reid, Councillor B. Butt, and Councillor N. Hart
ABSENT (1)
Councillor D. Damerla
Carried (10 to 0)

A Notice of Motion regarding Let's Play Ball and a proposed partnership model that would alleviate significant sport user fees for such initiatives (Councillor Dasko) (CONSENT)
1. Motion - Let's Play Ball January 5.pdf
RESOLUTION 0006-2026
Moved By Councillor S. Dasko
Seconded By Councillor B. Butt
WHEREAS recognizing that there is a growing demand for the City to provide athletic outdoor amenities in keeping with the principals of the City’s Future Directions plans;
AND WHEREAS recognizing there are affiliated groups which may want to work collaboratively with the City to help raise and build capital infrastructure;
AND WHEREAS activities such as cricket, baseball, soccer, football and the like as some examples maybe ideal groups to build this relationship with the City;
AND WHREAS this may provide the amenities for the City of Mississauga to help build these facilities more expeditiously as they would be removed from the City’s annual capital budgets;
THREREFORE staff be directed to bring a report back to Council on a proposed partnership model that would alleviate significant user fees for such initiatives.
