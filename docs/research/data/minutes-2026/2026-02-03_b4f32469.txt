DEPUTATIONS
7.1
Santhosh Durga Thyagarajan, Resident regarding wildlife diversity at Lake Aquitaine, issues and potential solutions
S. Thyagarajan outlined the different species inhabiting Lake Aquitaine, providing an overview of their 2.5 year study observing the wildlife and their impact to the area, highlighting concerns and opportunities for improvement.
Committee Members commented and inquired about the following:
Study method, hours spent, and who helped
Management of invasive species
Unique issues related to Lake Aquitaine
Signage related to feeding of wildlife
Opportunities for use of micro drone to study the area
S. Thyagarajan and Paul Tripodo, Supervisor, Natural Areas Management provided information related to inquires.
Councillor Mahoney, Chair spoke to opportunities to collaborate with local steward teams for better signage related to feeding of animals and noted he will be working with staff to look at additional signage in parks.
RECOMMENDATION EAC-0001-2026
Moved By Derek Stone
That the deputation and associated presentation from Santhosh Durga Thyagarajan, Resident regarding wildlife diversity at Lake Aquitaine, issues and potential solutions, be received.
Received
7.2
Andrea Dunn, Aquatic Ecologist, Credit Valley Conservation regarding Red Swamp Crayfish at Lake Aquitaine
A. Dunn spoke to the Credit River Watershed, outlining aquatic invasive species, and spoke to the various impacts and strategies to address the red swamp crayfish invasive species.
Committee Members commented and inquired about disposal of bait buckets at fishing sites, graphic and simple signage related to fishing, and opportunities for chemical and biological controls to address the red swamp crayfish.
In response, A. Dunn spoke to the work on signage and installations, and academic studies on biological and physical controls for invasive species.
RECOMMENDATION EAC-0002-2026
Moved By Alice Casselman
That the deputation and associated presentation from Andrea Dunn, Aquatic Ecologist, Credit Valley Conservation regarding Red Swamp Crayfish at Lake Aquitaine, be received.
Received
7.3
Donna Grantis, Resident regarding the Fossil Fuel Non-Proliferation Treaty
D. Grantis spoke to the Fossil Fuel Non-Proliferation Treaty, global initiatives, treaty goals, and alignment with Mississauga’s Climate Change Action Plan; and requested that Committee recommend the City formally endorse the treaty.
Councillor Fonseca spoke to D. Grantis’ Mississauga Walk of Fame induction, music achievements, arts and environment advocacy; and noted the City’s existing climate adaptation and mitigation actions aligned with the treaty goals.
Councillor Fonseca inquired about how action-oriented strategies could influence other levels of government. In response, D. Grantis spoke to local actions, such as green building and energy standards and ecosystem health to build momentum.
Councillor Dasko thanked D. Grantis for their deputation and spoke to the City’s climate emergency declaration and net-zero goals, and the Lakeview district energy.
Dianne Zimmerman, Manager, Environment spoke to meeting with Councillor Fonseca and D. Grantis regarding Councillor Fonseca’s draft motion on the endorsement of the treaty, and aligning with existing City motions and the Climate Change Action Plan.
RECOMMENDATION EAC-0003-2026
Moved By Councillor Dasko
That the deputation and associated presentation from Donna Grantis, Resident regarding the Fossil Fuel Non-Proliferation Treaty, be received.
Received
7.4
Paul Tripodo, Supervisor, Natural Areas Management regarding evidence based monitoring of spongy moth in Mississauga
P. Tripodo spoke to City’s monitoring program on spongy moth, outlining the approach, monitoring metrics, sampling methodology, and defoliation monitoring.
Committee Members engaged in discussion, commenting and inquiring about the following:
Outbreak timing, anticipation and response measures
Preventative measures (including at population level)
Coordination with bordering municipalities
Monitoring effectiveness of spraying
Cycle of spongy moth
In response, P. Tripodo provided information on enhancing monitoring, tools for residents, localized actions, coordination with municipalities, and monitoring effectiveness.
RECOMMENDATION EAC-0004-2026
Moved By Councillor Tedjo
That the deputation and associated presentation from Paul Tripodo, Supervisor, Natural Areas Management regarding evidence based monitoring of spongy moth in Mississauga, be received.
Received
