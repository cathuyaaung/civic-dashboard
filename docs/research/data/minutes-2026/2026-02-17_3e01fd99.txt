10.2
Update for the Dixie-Dundas Flood Mitigation Project
Committee Members engaged in discussion, commenting and inquiring about the following:
Mitigation funding received for the Dixie–Dundas Flood Mitigation Project
Whether any funding applications remain outstanding
Level of flooding the project is designed to protect against
When residents can expect project completion and reduced flood risk
Project’s target schedule and a clear completion date
Concerns about student safety at St. Edmund School given potential flooding through the school yard
Detailed schedule review of project
Clearer communication about timelines and potential project delays, and
Letter to school board requesting St. Edmund School’s emergency plan.
In response, Scott Perry, Manager, Stormwater Assets & Programming, Lincoln Kan, Manager, Environmental Services, and Emma Calvert, Director, Infrastructure Planning & Engineering Services responded with information related to funding, press release, project stages and target schedule, and engagement with St. Edmund School.
Councillor Dasko spoke to the St. Edmund School walk through following the second major storm and suggested that a formal letter be sent to the school board requesting information about the school’s emergency plan.
Councillor Fonseca, Chair spoke in support of sending the letter to the school and spoke to a potential request at Council for the Mayor to send thank-you letters to all levels of government for funding received.
Direction was provided to Transportation and Works staff to provide a detailed update to the Stormwater Advisory Committee at its next meeting regarding the Dixie-Dundas Flood Mitigation Project status of design, schedule, construction stages, implementation, and flood-risk reduction benefits.
RECOMMENDATION SWAC-0003-2026
Moved By Mike Gaudet
That the Chair of the Stormwater Advisory Committee send a letter to the Dufferin-Peel Catholic District School Board with respect to the St. Edmund School evacuation process and that the City's Emergency Management Group and the School Board Trustee be copied on this letter.
Approved

10.3
Stormwater management required for development and ditch management in the City
Councillor Hart spoke to the Rain Ready Home Guide raising concerns overs its usability for non-English speakers and suggested the content be created as a webpage and residents can use the Google Translate function. In response, Scott Perry, Manager, Stormwater Assets & Programming noted the suggestion will be taken back for review.
Committee Members engaged in discussion, commenting and inquiring about the following:
Whether proactive monitoring using ortho‑imagery is used to identify public encroachments into the City’s drainage system
How much of aerial monitoring is focused on stormwater issues
Whether the City tracks changes in hard surfaces and impervious areas over time
How the City ensures new developments comply with stormwater controls after construction
What the post‑construction inspection and compliance process looks like
Whether the City is coordinating with Metrolinx regarding the Dundas Street dedicated bus lane plans and their connection to the Dixie‑Dundas Flood Mitigation Project
Whether Metrolinx could potentially fund any bridge‑related work on Dundas Street
Issues relating to the Dundas BRT project and bridge infrastructure
In response, S. Perry and Emma Calvert, Director, Infrastructure Planning & Engineering Services provided information addressing Committee inquiries.
Councillor Fonseca, Chair asked about opportunity to include educational inserts for utility bills (stormwater charge) noting responsibilities, who to call, and the breakdown of the stormwater dollars. In response, S. Perry noted that the City used inserts in the past and that staff can look into the opportunity.
Direction was provided to Transportation and Works staff to follow-up with funding information distinguishing the components funded under the transit programs and stormwater and flood-mitigation programs as well as the roles of each of the involved parties.
RECOMMENDATION SWAC-0005-2026
Moved By Councillor N. Hart
That the memorandum from Scott Perry, Manager, Stormwater Assets & Programming, dated February 6, 2026 and entitled “Stormwater management required for development and ditch management in the City”, be received.
Received

12.
OTHER BUSINESS
Paul Bunyard, Citizen Member asked if there has been an analysis on whether creek banks could overtop and flood homes along the main street in Lisgar, gives the upstream changes and shifts since the area’s original design.
Lincoln Kan, Manager, Environmental Services spoke to the Conservation Authorities undertaking flood plain analysis using advanced modeling technology to update flood lines noting City staff are included on the committee undertaking the work.
P. Bunyard further inquired about existing flood mapping and asked about the responsibility for monitoring changes and protecting residents.
L. Kan responded spoke to the municipal responsibility of managing stormwater and Conservation Authorities.
Councillor Fonseca, Chair spoke to the Planning Act and Conservation Authorities’ role in reviewing development applications.
Councillor Fonseca, Chair requested that Conservation Authorities be invited to present to Committee and L. Kan confirmed that Credit Valley Conservation and Toronto and Region Conservation Authority would be presenting to Committee at its next meeting. Mike Gaudet, Citizen Member suggested Committee provide questions to the Conservation Authorities in advance.
Direction was provided to Transportation and Works staff for the next Committee meeting to bring an action list tracking the Committee’s achievements and upcoming actionable items.
Councillor Fonseca, Chair requested clarification on public and closed session meetings; in response, Sacha Smith, Manager, Secretariat and Access & Privacy spoke to the Municipal Act, 2001 public requirement and exceptions for closed sessions.
Councillor Fonseca, Chair spoke to forming a subcommittee for site visits to stormwater infrastructure and S. Smith spoke to the requirements and process of forming a subcommittee.
P. Bunyard noted that subcommittee could also hold technical meetings with staff on issues, not limited to site visits.
RECOMMENDATION SWAC-0006-2026
Moved By Paul Bunyard
That a Subcommittee of the Stormwater Advisory Committee to undertake site visits to City-wide stormwater infrastructure and discuss technical matters related to flood control, be established.
Approved
